## Concepts
- [[product-operating-model]] — 🌳 the term's definitional home: a model (principles), not a process; the four cuts (20 principles · 3 dimensions · 5 concepts · 4 competencies) through one thing. Complements [[overview]].
- [[empowered-product-teams]] — 📝 durable cross-functional team given problems, not features; the most fundamental concept.
- [[outcome-based-roadmap]] — 📝 the transitional artifact between feature-roadmaps and direct strategy/objectives communication; outcomes-as-entries with high-integrity-commitment escape hatches.
- [[the-four-big-risks]] — 📝 value, usability, feasibility, viability (ethics inside viability); the risks discovery addresses.
- [[product-vision]] — 📝 the customer-improving future you're creating; mission dropped in TRANSFORMED.
- [[product-strategy]] — 📝 one holistic strategy = choosing the most important problems to solve.
- [[team-objectives]] — 📝 a significant problem + measures of success; maps to OKRs.
- [[product-principles]] — 📝 a company's *own* written beliefs that guide product trade-offs (one of the six strategic-context elements). **Not** the model's 20 first principles — those describe the model; these are an artifact your leaders author.
- [[product-discovery]] — 📝 figuring out what to build by testing risks cheaply; output = validated backlog.
- [[product-delivery]] — 📝 building & shipping reliably and often; engineering-owned.
- [[product-culture]] — 📝 trust/learning/innovation over control/blame/predictability.
- [[feature-teams-vs-product-teams]] — 📝 the two axes orgs confuse: *empowerment* (problems vs. features) and *durability* (product vs. project). Both must be right.
- [[coaching-agency]] — 📝 Idiodi's coined framing (*Product Therapy* Ep 1): agency = *do people feel they can change their circumstances?* — the prerequisite behind [[empowered-product-teams|empowered teams]] and [[missionaries-vs-mercenaries|missionaries]]. Three root causes of low agency (want / worth / fear); the tip-of-the-week script (*"that's a great idea / what problem / how will we succeed?"*).
- [[imposter-syndrome]] — 📝 Chris Jones on *Product Therapy* Ep 6: healthy dose (do your homework) vs paralyzing dose (over-preparation, faking through, avoidance). Coach *into* the fear, not around it. The "sign-up-for-six" hack; upward reciprocity as service.
- [[product-ethics]] — 📝 Jon Moore on *Product Therapy* Ep 5: ethics as an emerging **fifth risk** alongside value/usability/feasibility/viability. The PM at the frontline; psychological safety as enabling mechanism; diversity as upstream. The eight reflection questions for a specific decision.
- [[missionaries-vs-mercenaries]] — 📝 missionaries believe in the vision and own the outcome; mercenaries build what's told. Engineers are the tell.
- [[empowered-engineers]] — 📝 "the most important thing": engineers given problem + strategic context. The single largest source of innovation.
- [[time-to-money]] — 📝 the right metric for product teams; replaces time-to-market in the project model.
- [[team-topology]] — 📝 how the org splits work across teams. "Loosely coupled, highly aligned." Head of product + head of tech own the call.
- [[product-coaching]] — 📝 the primary mechanism by which PMs learn the model; SVPG's Feb 2026 diagnosis: the supply gap is the industry's primary obstacle.
- [[model-as-product-coach]] — 📝 Cagan's Feb 2026 prescription: foundation models + project files + project instructions + strategic context as a personal product coach. This wiki is one implementation.
- [[coaching-vs-contracting]] — 📝 a coach teaches you to do the work; it never does it for you. The scalability hinge — and the line that separates model-as-coach from PM theater.
- [[types-of-product-coaching]] — 📝 discovery / leadership / transformation coaching, across startup / scaleup / enterprise clients; how to locate what kind of help a user needs.
- [[transformation]] — 📝 the multi-year shift from project/IT/feature-factory to the product model; three dimensions (build/solve/decide); pilot-team approach; Moore-vs-Cagan sequencing tension captured.
- [[transformation-politics]] — 📝 the eight political dimensions Cagan names; trust stakeholders on the problem; hold *Decide* for last; monthly drumbeat of outcomes.
- [[transformation-as-a-project]] — 📝 named anti-pattern: running the transformation in the very mode you're trying to leave. Use the product model to transform.
- [[the-need-for-speed]] — 📝 Cagan's 10 reasons speed matters beyond time-to-market: learning, MVP focus, quality, morale, the only sustainable differentiator.
- [[product-management-theater]] — 📝 the title without the job; the reckoning; three models; the agile-theater sibling. AI both exposes and accelerates it.
- [[founder-style-leadership]] — 📝 Cagan's reframe of "Founder Mode": product sense + coaching — not micromanagement, not hands-off delegation. Matters more as you scale.
- [[product-sense]] — 📝 the judgment that powers good product decisions; *earned* by doing your homework, not innate; AI accelerates building it fast.
- [[build-to-learn-vs-build-to-earn]] — 📝 Jeff Patton's distinction: discovery (prototype to learn) vs delivery (production to earn); different tools, different testing.
- [[data-product-management]] — 📝 the product model applied to data/AI/platform work; data-to-decide vs data-to-power; the "is it a product?" litmus test; platform PMs; customer-facing vs customer-enabling teams.
- [[process-people]] — 📝 the third contributor type Cagan names in 2021 (alongside makers and managers); the four reasons hiring them usually causes harm; Europe = PO problem, US = product-ops problem; how the six product-ops models map to the general phenomenon.
- [[strategic-context]] — 📝 the org's own baseline (mission, scorecard, objectives, vision, strategy, principles) empowered teams **and AI agents** need. Org-authored, maintained by the org, supplied to agents **separately** — this org-agnostic wiki teaches *about* it, never stores one.
- [[cross-functional-partnering]] — 📝 the Part VI hub: how empowered product teams interact with Customers · Sales · Product Marketing · Finance · Stakeholders · Executives. The "what healthy looks like" complement to [[transformation-politics]] and [[overcoming-objections]].
- [[corporate-courage]] — 📝 the leadership disposition that separates transforming leaders from ambivalent ones. Named as Key #10 in [[transformed|TRANSFORMED]] Ch 48; also present as Reason #9 in Cagan's older "weak product" list.
- [[coaching-mindset]] — 📝 the seven-principle mindset a manager/coach must hold *before* the coaching mechanics (assessment · plan · 1:1 · narrative) can work. Book-length primary: [[empowered|EMPOWERED]] Ch 7 + the opening [[bill-campbell|Campbell]] mentors-vs-coaches passage.
- [[pmm-ambassador]] — 📝 [[product-marketing|PMM]] Fundamental 1 (LOVED Ch 3): Connect Customer + Market Insights. The 11 market-sensing questions; Third-Party Insights; Competition-as-chess; insights ≠ requirements (PM/PMM boundary).
- [[pmm-strategist]] — 📝 PMM Fundamental 2 (LOVED Ch 4): Direct the product's go-to-market. The Key Terms disambiguation (GTM engine · marketing strategy · GTM models · product go-to-market); 6 GTM models; 6 strategy themes; maturity arc.
- [[pmm-storyteller]] — 📝 PMM Fundamental 3 (LOVED Ch 5): Positioning (long game) + messaging (short game). Formulas as input not output; CAST guide; Looker vs RJMetrics; the 70% dark-funnel framing.
- [[pmm-evangelist]] — 📝 PMM Fundamental 4 (LOVED Ch 6): *"The systematic enabling of influence through others."* Quizlet + Word cases; enable champions not just reps; Evangelism ≠ Promotion Table 6.1; communities.
- [[pmm-pm-partnership]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 8 (PMM-side view; complements TRANSFORMED Ch 23): the *"quad"* framing; ratio question (avg 1:2.5); 5 alignment patterns; 3 anti-patterns; weekly/bimonthly/quarterly cadence.
- [[pmm-marketing-partnership]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 9. Workiva SEC event opener; where PMM ends and marketing begins; 4 marketing-goal → activity map; 3 anti-patterns.
- [[pmm-sales-partnership]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 10. Paper-data-sheet cautionary tale; sales urgent vs PMM right natural tension; reference customers + sales playbook as the two most powerful tools; 11 key activities.
- [[market-fit]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 11. *"Discovering market pull."* The market side of PMF as its own concept (distinct from product-market fit). Word-for-lawyers case; Value + Growth/Connection question sets; 4 existing-product rediscovery techniques.
- [[technology-adoption-life-cycle]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 15. The 5-segment bell curve; 7–10 year timeline; Superhuman precise-early-adopter example; the overgeneralized-segment trap; iPhone decade walkthrough.
- [[brand-lever]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 16. Brand-as-promise reframe; the 4 PMM brand situations; 3 brand levels; Salesforce Marketing Cloud naming taxonomy; Netscape/AOL cautionary tale.
- [[pricing-lever]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 17. *"Pricing is value engineering."* Who owns pricing (monetization/pricing/packaging); 4 essential pricing-metric concepts; the 3 canonical packaging strategies.
- [[positioning]] — 📝 LOVED Chs 21 + 24. Brendan/RSA opener; positioning takes time (Microsoft Office); the timing balancing act; *"competition isn't a crowd, it's confirmation"*; create-new vs redefine-existing category kings.
- [[messaging]] — 📝 LOVED Chs 22 + 23. Expensify *"expense reports don't suck"*; Expensify vs SAP Concur X-ray; **CAST deep-dive**; Netflix (2009 → 2014 → 2016) and Zendesk (2010–11 → 2014 → post-IPO) messaging-evolution walkthroughs.
- [[product-marketing-by-stage]] — 📝 LOVED Chs 29 + 30. Roadster/Michelle Denogean opener; Early/Growth/Mature staging with the mature-company competitive-response worked example; 4 mature-company inflection points (traditional-to-tech-first · single-to-multi-product · product-to-solution · international expansion).
- [[continuous-discovery]] — 📝 discovery run as a weekly cadence, in parallel with delivery, by the same team. Carries the Dual-Track-Agile → Continuous-Discovery naming lineage; Torres's four-clause operational test.
- [[insights]] — 📝 the four sources (quantitative · qualitative · technology · industry) that power strategy; evaluative vs. generative; insights must be shared, not just discovered. The mechanics behind the [[powered-by-insights]] principle.
- [[psychological-safety]] — 📝 the shared team belief that it's safe to speak up, offer ideas, admit uncertainty, and make mistakes without negative repercussions. Grounded primarily in Kate Leto on *Product Therapy* Ep 22 (Google Project Aristotle; trust ≠ PS; EQ enables PS; hero-complex blocker; leaders modeling *"I don't know"*; the rehearsed call-out technique), with book-canon grounding from *TRANSFORMED* itself (the remote-team "psychological safety is dropping" diagnostic; disagreement as a healthy sign of safety).
- [[ai-and-product-teams]] — 📝 how GenAI reshapes empowered product teams over a 2–5 year horizon. Primary root: Cagan on *Product Therapy* Ep 20 (May 2025). The four historical product problems; discovery/delivery balance flips to ~90/10; PM harder + more essential; tech lead absorbs ML/data role; team topology improves; the AI-tools-restore-remote-discovery-friction bet.
- [[remote-collaboration]] — 📝 how empowered product teams do the collaborative half of their work when they aren't in the same room. Primary root: Hickman on *Product Therapy* Ep 15. Rules of proximity · always-on with self-view off · false-trust trap · collaborating-through-an-artifact anti-pattern · the huddle metaphor · the-best-collaboration-tool-is-the-phone · the leader-level "tie VP objectives to shared outcomes" HR fix.
- [[product-in-startups]] — 📝 how product work is different — and often harder — inside a pre-PMF startup. Primary root: Lauchengco on *Product Therapy* Ep 16. Ideas are dime-a-dozen · **market-product fit** (not the reverse) · 9–10x adoption bar · founder gap-closer trait · co-founder rule 100% · opportunistic first hire · joining-a-startup 4 assessments.
- [[coaching-up]] — 📝 the IC's craft of developing their own manager — exposing information the leader might not see so behavior can change, without a career-progression motive. Primary root: Hickman on *Product Therapy* Ep 27. Coaching-up vs managing-up distinction; if-your-manager-doesn't-coach-you sequence (ask directly · model the framework · celebrate when they try); jiu-jitsu for the always-right manager (ask for help); name-the-pattern with positive intent for the checked-out manager; Idiodi's **User Manual** technique (both write-one-about-your-manager and give-your-reports-one-about-you).
- [[transformation-anti-patterns]] — 📝 the wiki's canonical named catalog of transformation anti-patterns. Primary root: Castenfors on *Product Therapy* Ep 38 (author of *10 Anti-Patterns When Moving to the Product Model*), with Hickman on Ep 37. Includes the *loop of transformation failure* (six-step), the *air sandwich* (vision above, backlog below, gap in the middle), *17 North Stars*, *transformation in a bubble*, *autonomy too soon*, the *three value buckets* (new / existing / protect), pilot theater / pilot-team factory / sacred golden child, *dependencies are the root of all evil*.
- [[cultural-debt]] — 📝 the hidden cost of neglecting the system that governs how people work together — analog to tech debt; interest compounds silently. Primary root: Fisher on *Product Therapy* Ep 43. *What you reward always beats what you say* (Wells Fargo); wartime rationalizations become the culture; silence in rooms + "what he really meant" translation work as signals; Boeing (decades → 737 Max crashes → Alaska Airlines door bolts years later) as the long-tail example; blameless postmortem for cultural failures; **culture is a C-suite responsibility, not an HR problem**; avoidance disguised as kindness ("No More Mr. Nice Guy").
- [[keep-the-lights-on]] — 📝 maintenance / compliance / bug-fixing / tech-debt work — separate from and not stopped by strategic focus ("would be suicide" — Cagan). Castenfors's three-buckets frame (new value / existing value / protect value); Idiodi's first rule of tech debt: talk about tech debt.

## Competencies
- [[product-manager]] — 📝 owns value & viability risk; the people/process/product skill taxonomy (weak vs strong).
- [[product-designer]] — 📝 owns usability risk + the holistic customer experience; the five attributes of the modern designer (Jones, 2016).
- [[engineers]] — 📝 the Tech Lead + engineers; own feasibility + delivery; "the most important thing" — the single largest source of innovation.
- [[product-leadership]] — 📝 managers of PM/design/eng; management (coaching + staffing) + leadership (the five elements of strategic context). Empowered teams need *better* leadership, not less.
- [[the-product-team-trio]] — 📝 PM + designer + Tech Lead as peers, owning the four risks together. The competency core of every empowered team.
- [[delivery-manager]] — 📝 the role Cagan rebranded from "project manager"; clears impediments and tracks high-integrity commitments so the PM can do discovery.
- [[product-coach]] — 📝 develops product people; the experience prerequisite, the three coaching types, the business of coaching.
- [[product-ops]] — 📝 best as a *force multiplier* (user research + analytics + best-practices/tools), not governance or a band-aid for weak management; the six models; "lipstick on a pig" when done wrong.
- [[product-marketing]] — 📝 the market side of product-market fit (positioning, go-to-market, enablement); Lauchengco's *LOVED*; the partner a PM most needs with a direct sales channel.
- [[leading-product-marketing]] — 📝 LOVED Chs 26–28. Where PMM reports (2 factors); role scope by stage; Project Aristotle 5 factors; the signature "great marketing company" hiring question; the 4 Director-to-VP shifts (from Lauchengco's "I'm a Great Director of Marketing" essay).

## Frameworks
- [[coaching-the-pm]] — 📝 the PM coaching curriculum: how to develop each skill in the people/process/product taxonomy (Cagan's "The Plan").
- [[opportunity-assessment]] — 📝 the canonical 10-question Cagan framework for deciding whether to pursue a product idea, lightweight enough to live on a page.
- [[high-integrity-commitments]] — 📝 how to honor date-based commitments while operating an outcome-based team; the give-and-take of discovery-window-for-confident-date.
- [[pilot-teams]] — 📝 the canonical SVPG transformation technique: hand-picked trio, meaningful business problem, training + coaching, drumbeat of outcomes. Risk-mitigation tool + political mechanism + proof-of-concept.
- [[it-to-product-organization]] — 📝 Cagan's 10-step framework (2008, still load-bearing) for evolving a legacy IT org into a product org; the structural changes a CIO-led tech org must make.
- [[prototypes]] — 📝 the four prototype types (low-fi/hi-fi simulation, live-data, feasibility); the build-to-learn artifact; never commit a date without a feasibility prototype.
- [[hiring-and-onboarding]] — 📝 first product hire (~25 engineers); hire for potential; assessment → coaching plan → competence (~3 months); domain expertise vs domain dogma.
- [[okrs]] — 📝 the SVPG way to run OKRs / team objectives: an *empowerment* technique; objective + 2 KRs (primary + backstop, targets from the team); the three prerequisites; roof/moon shot; high-integrity commitments; weekly check-ins; accountability; the "OKR theater" anti-patterns.
- [[partnering-with-executives]] — 📝 the Ch 26 six-element trust model (Decisions · Outcomes · Disagreements · Promises · Surprises · Trust) as a coaching artifact for the product-leader ↔ executive relationship.
- [[keys-to-successful-transformation]] — 📝 Cagan & Hickman's ten keys (Ch 48): CEO · Role of Technology · Strong Product Leaders · True PMs · Professional Designers · Empowered Engineers · Insights-Based Strategy · Stakeholder Collaboration · Continuous Evangelization of Outcomes · Corporate Courage. Includes the "But What Can I Do?" bottom-up appendix and the wiki's dependency structure across the ten.
- [[reference-customer-program]] — 📝 the ~6–8 target-market customers who partner with the trio through the whole product effort and become public reference customers at launch. Cagan's book-form origin: [[inspired|INSPIRED]] Ch 15 *Charter User Programs* (1st ed, 2008); modern canon: [[transformed|TRANSFORMED]] Ch 23 *Customer Discovery Program*. Non-negotiables: no prepayment · target market not early adopters · not a custom build · partners not customers-yet. Platform variant → 6 reference *applications*; consumer variant → 10–15.
- [[product-discovery-techniques]] — 📝 the hub / catalog of Cagan's discovery techniques (grounded in [[inspired|INSPIRED]] Part IV, 2nd ed 2017): framing (opportunity assessment · customer letter · startup canvas) → planning (story map · customer discovery program) → ideation (customer interviews · concierge test · customer misbehavior · hack days) → prototyping (the four types) → testing (feasibility · usability · value — demand / qual / quant · viability) → transformation (discovery sprint · pilot team). Includes the "how to pick" algorithm and the ethics-as-separate-risk lineage note.
- [[opportunity-solution-tree]] — 📝 [[teresa-torres|Torres]]'s visual framework: outcome → opportunities → solutions → assumption tests. Compare-and-contrast, not whether-or-not. The stakeholder-management artifact ([[continuous-discovery-habits|CDH]] Chs 2, 6, 7, 13). Complement to [[opportunity-assessment|Cagan's 10 questions]] — use both.
- [[continuous-interviewing]] — 📝 [[teresa-torres|Torres]]'s **keystone habit** ([[continuous-discovery-habits|CDH]] Chs 5, 14): at least one story-based interview per week, by the trio, on target-market users. Feeds the [[opportunity-solution-tree|OST]]'s opportunity space. Cadence, direct-questions-are-broken evidence, excavation technique, interview snapshots.
- [[assumption-testing]] — 📝 [[teresa-torres|Torres]]'s discipline ([[continuous-discovery-habits|CDH]] Chs 9, 10): don't test whole ideas — enumerate the leap-of-faith assumptions each idea depends on and test those. **Five categories** — desirability · viability · feasibility · usability · **ethical** (kept separate, aligned with Cagan's pre-2017 framing; see [[the-four-big-risks]] lineage note). Story-map → assumption map → simulate-experience-evaluate-behavior → small first, iterate to larger.
- [[discovery-techniques-catalog]] — 📝 **Supporting reference (non-SVPG)** — [[anders-toxboe|Toxboe]] / [[learning-loop|Learning Loop]] 89-play catalog remapped into Cagan's four-risks frame. Sorted descending by evidence strength within each risk. Every row tagged with a Cagan alignment (Endorsed · Compatible · Use-with-care) + one-line summary + link to the play page on learningloop.io. Complements [[product-discovery-techniques|the SVPG-canon hub]] as a broader menu when the ~20 canon techniques don't fit.
- [[agile-product-marketing]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 12. The **Release Scale** (shared vocabulary between product + GTM teams, 7-step recipe) + **Agile Marketing** (6 principles + weekly-scrum mechanics). How PMM stays aligned with continuous-delivery product teams.
- [[product-marketing-metrics]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 13. 5 example PMM OKRs; OKR/KR/KPI disambiguation with the Sales-Cycle-Time worked example; the four-category metric stack (product · marketing · sales · financial); the diagnostic chain from business metric back through the PMM stack.
- [[one-sheet-gtm-canvas]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 19. The lightweight one-page artifact that aligns product + marketing + sales + CS on a product's GTM. Bandwidth walkthrough; 4 swim lanes; 6-step recipe; SWOT gut check; Table 19.1 company-first vs customer-first.
- [[one-sheet-messaging-canvas]] — 📝 LOVED Ch 25. The building-blocks artifact for messaging discovery + final messaging. 7-step process; IndexTank worked example; CAST final check; the FAQ.
- [[product-evangelism]] — 📝 INSPIRED Ch 31. *"Selling the dream"* (Guy Kawasaki). The PM's essential communication craft; **10 techniques** (use a prototype · share the pain · share the vision · share learnings · share credit · learn to give a great demo · do your homework · be genuinely excited · show enthusiasm · spend time with the team). Internal-facing complement to [[pmm-evangelist]].

- [[time-management]] — 📝 Chris Jones on *Product Therapy* Ep 11: the Eisenhower matrix; the pie-chart exercise; the four types of meetings (status · decide · solve problem · build relationship); Idiodi's seven meeting rules; discovery-as-day-job (~4 hrs); the three cultural drivers of a meetings culture (low trust · no context · no discovery).
- [[product-career-development]] — 📝 Jon Moore on *Product Therapy* Ep 9: approach-your-career-as-a-product (vision · strategy · competencies per stage); Kate Leto's **human skills** reframe (EI · collaborative problem-solving · critical thinking · trust · agency · time · perseverance); life-experience as an asset; mentorship as leverage; *don't rush team-level jobs*.
- [[decision-making]] — 📝 Lauchengco on *Product Therapy* Ep 19: team-and-PM-level decision making distinct from executive-partnering. - [[model-maturity]] — 📝 whole-org rollup against the three transformation dimensions (build / solve / decide). Project model → exemplary.
- [[outcome-vs-output-orientation]] — 📝 do you celebrate shipping or moving the KPI? The behavioral test of empowerment.
