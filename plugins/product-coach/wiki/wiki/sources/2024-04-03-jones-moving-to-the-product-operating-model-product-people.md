# Jones — Transformed: Moving to a Product Operating Model (Product People)

> **Citation:** Chris Jones (SVPG), *Transformed: Moving to a Product Operating Model*, Product People weekly livestream · YouTube, published 2024-04-03 (three weeks after *TRANSFORMED*'s release) — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vRplb-ytSGo). Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** The cleanest ~35-minute spoken walkthrough of the model by a *TRANSFORMED* co-author: what an operating model *is* at all, the three dimensions, the four competencies, and — its distinctive contribution to this wiki — the **[[john-deere-smart-industrial|John Deere]]** story as the worked example of a 190-year-old non-tech company operating the model. Root source is [[transformed]]; this talk is the introduction to it.

## Notes for the coach
- **Jones's two-part answer to "how do you measure a transformation?"** — (1) your ordinary **business outcomes**, and whether you can even attribute them to product work (most companies pre-transformation cannot); (2) the qualitative one: **what can you do now that you could not have conceived of before?** This is the same litmus test [[2022-04-13-cagan-common-transformation-pitfalls-producttank-oslo|Cagan uses at ProductTank Oslo]], arrived at independently in the same corpus — worth citing together.
- **On planning cadence:** strategy revisited annually with quarterly adjustment is fine; what comes out of planning should be *focus areas and problems*, not release content.
- **Tool agnosticism:** SVPG deliberately declines to endorse tools or methodologies; the risk is the tool becoming the thing. Pairs with [[2018-07-26-cagan-tools-and-processes]].
- Includes the *"where does your company need to be in five years?"* framing, illustrated with the Uber 2012–2017 five-year window and then generative AI. Backs [[disruption-response]].
