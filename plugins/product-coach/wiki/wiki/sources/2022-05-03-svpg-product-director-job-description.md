# SVPG — Product Director Job Description

> **Citation:** SVPG · "Product Director Job Description" · svpg.com, 2022-05-03 — [read the original](https://www.svpg.com/product-director-job-description/). Slim provenance card; the article is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** SVPG's **template job description** for a director of product management at a medium-to-large company (where a VP Product / CPO owns vision + strategy, and directors own coaching + staffing the ICs). Companion to [[2022-05-03-cagan-the-vp-product-role|The VP Product Role]] (senior) and [[2022-05-03-svpg-example-pm-job-description|Example PM Job Description]] (IC) — the three SVPG-authored JDs form a full ladder.

## What it backs
- competencies: [[product-leadership]] — the operational form of the director-tier product-leader role: primary responsibility is **staffing and coaching strong PMs**, not managing products directly. Reinforces the *EMPOWERED* Ch 3 claim that first-level people managers are "the primary culprit" behind weak product companies.
- frameworks: [[product-coaching]] — one of the necessary qualifications is *"demonstrate a clear understanding of your role as the primary coach of your product managers, and how that differs from managing products directly."* The JD calls this out as a distinct interview criterion.
- concepts: [[trust-over-control]] — *"Demonstrate servant-based leadership and knowledge of leading by context rather than control"* — the operational criterion. The JD encodes "context, not control" as a hiring bar.
- competencies: [[product-manager]] — the JD restates the PM's own value + viability accountability (context for what the director is coaching *toward*).
- entities: [[svpg]], [[marty-cagan]].

## Notes for the coach
- **The Ben Horowitz claim.** *"Ben Horowitz argues that the director of product management is the most important non-C level position in a tech company. We agree."* This is the article's opening — a strong, quotable Cagan endorsement of the director-tier role. Use it when a company is deciding whether to hire directors of PM at all, or considering absorbing that layer into the VP.
- **The three-tier SVPG JD set is a coherent hiring frame.** [[2022-05-03-svpg-example-pm-job-description|IC PM]] (owns value + viability) → **Director** (this article — owns coaching, staffing, and quarterly problem assignment) → [[2022-05-03-cagan-the-vp-product-role|VP Product / CPO]] (owns vision + strategy + culture + team development at senior scale). Recommend all three together when a company is standing up (or resetting) its product org.
- **Assigning objectives is director-tier.** The JD names *"assisting with quarterly planning and assigning objectives (problems to solve) to product teams"* as an explicit director responsibility — corroborates *EMPOWERED* Ch 3 which puts objective-setting in the management column.
- **Coaching, not managing products.** The JD repeatedly frames the shift from IC → director as a *change of subject*: from managing the product to managing the people who manage products. Coaching aptitude ("proven track record of coaching and developing other people") is called out as a **necessary qualification**, not a nice-to-have.
- **Small-org caveat.** The framing is explicitly medium-to-large (VP Product owns vision + strategy). In smaller orgs where the VP role is absent, the director frequently owns vision/strategy too — see [[2022-05-03-cagan-the-vp-product-role|The VP Product Role]] for the senior form.
