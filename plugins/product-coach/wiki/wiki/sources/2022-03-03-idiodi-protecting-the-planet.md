# Protecting the Planet, One Customer at a Time

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] · *Protecting the Planet, One Customer at a Time* · SVPG, Mar 3, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/protecting-the-planet-one-customer-at-a-time/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — Idiodi's John Deere transformation article**, complementary to [[2024-04-03-jones-moving-to-the-product-operating-model-product-people|Jones's Product People talk]] which is the currently-cited root for [[john-deere-smart-industrial]]. Idiodi's article predates the Jones talk by two years and is the earliest wiki-attested SVPG-canonical account of John Deere's transformation.

Key material:
- **The 185-year legacy.** Founded 1837 (John Deere invents the steel plow). Followed by horse-drawn reapers, sulky plows, mowers, threshing machines. Each innovation transformed the agricultural industry.
- **The 2022 challenge.** Global population expected to reach 9.6 billion by 2050. Farmers asking John Deere for more than equipment — they want profitability + environmental responsibility.
- **The transformation.** From equipment manufacturer → technology company with "smart farming" at the center of operations.
- **The vision.** "Protecting the planet, one customer at a time" — the framing that oriented a legacy manufacturer's move to product.
