# Cagan & Olsen — EMPOWERED Fireside Chat (Lean Product Meetup)

> **Citation:** Marty Cagan in conversation with Dan Olsen · *EMPOWERED: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products* launch fireside, Lean Product Meetup webinar, recorded December 3, 2020 · YouTube, published February 18, 2021 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iKYwUOFhjEs). Cagan's **5th** Lean Product Meetup appearance (see [[2026-07-22-cagan-olsen-how-ai-impacts-product-management|the 10th]]). Slim provenance card; the transcript is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon) — reinforcement, with the clearest statement of how [[inspired|INSPIRED]] and [[empowered|EMPOWERED]] divide the work**, plus the book's own origin story.

Key material:

(a) **The two books, in one sentence each — the division to cite.** *"**INSPIRED** is all about how an empowered team works. If your company doesn't allow your teams to work that way and your company wants to become that, then **the leaders need to change how they work** — and so **EMPOWERED** is really meant to answer the question of **how do we provide the environment that these teams need** in order to work that way."*

(b) **The book had an MVP.** An earlier article was, in his words, *"really an MVP for the book — I wanted to see how people responded to that message."* The response (*"a lot of people told me this was really needed for them; they didn't understand how to do this"*) is what committed him to writing it. Worth noting as Cagan applying his own [[build-to-learn-vs-build-to-earn|build-to-learn]] discipline to a book.

(c) **Written in the first COVID lockdown.** *"I had nothing else to do for several months. I couldn't travel… I was even quarantining from my family because I had come off a business trip right when the pandemic started. So I had nothing to do but write — and it's a good way to take your mind off crazy politics and a crazy pandemic."* Just over 400 pages.

- **(a) is the piece to reuse.** When someone asks which SVPG book to read, this is Cagan's own answer to the division: INSPIRED for how the team works, EMPOWERED for how leaders create the conditions, and *TRANSFORMED* (three years later) for how you get from one model to the other.
