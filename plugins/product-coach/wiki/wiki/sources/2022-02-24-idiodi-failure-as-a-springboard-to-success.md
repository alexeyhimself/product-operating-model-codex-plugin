# Failure as a Springboard to Success

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] · *Failure as a Springboard to Success* · SVPG, Feb 24, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/failure-as-a-springboard-to-success/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the *early failure* chapter of Idiodi's Datasite transformation** cluster, opening with an Edison analogy (Pearl Street power station, Sept 4, 1882). Third of the three Idiodi Datasite articles in this batch (with [[2022-02-24-idiodi-the-freedom-to-grow|The Freedom to Grow]] and [[2023-06-02-idiodi-breaking-down-walls|Breaking Down Walls]]).

Key material:
- **Edison hook.** Sept 4, 1882 — Edison flipped the switch on his Pearl Street power station. What if the Times Building had remained dark? Would any company survive such a high-profile failure?
- **Datasite context.** Previously operated as Merrill Corporation (paper-based financial printer). Clients moved to the Cloud → Merrill had to too. Datasite as a platform had existed since 2001 but was woefully outdated.
- **The failure driver.** *"The problem was that the rebuild was driven not from a shared vision but from a sense of urgency. The company did not want to lose customers."*
- **The lesson.** Urgency ≠ vision. Rebuild driven by fear-of-loss (not by "what better thing can we build") led to early failure. The successful rebuild came only after a proper vision was articulated.
