# Product Therapy Ep 11 — Coaching Time Management

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[chris-jones|Chris Jones]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 11 · SVPG, December 19, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-Vzgssupic). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** The wiki's canonical time-management episode. Structure: **Eisenhower matrix** (urgent × important; urgent always wins by default, and quadrant-2 *important-but-not-urgent* work — discovery, especially — is what disappears); **the pie-chart exercise** (get the PM to draw the slices of last week the way it *actually* was, not the way they wished it was — then coach the gap); **Chris Jones's 4 types of meetings** (communicate status · make a decision · solve a problem · build relationship — the fourth is remote-work-only and needs deliberate protection); **the "sales-hero PM" story** (a PM whose "customer time" was 100% in-deal, 0% out-of-deal — the pie chart made it visible). Idiodi's **7 meeting rules** (culture play): (1) never default to an hour — start at 20 min; (2) start on odd times (8:47, 1:55) not the top of the hour; (3) require written narrative before you call one (Amazon 6-pager); (4) cap attendees around 8 (Bain: each additional attendee costs ~10% decision effectiveness); (5) an agenda + a purpose or **decline**; (6) meetings ≠ work — talking became work; (7) the meeting *problem* is downstream of low trust + low context + no discovery. **Discovery = day job for PMs and designers; side hustle for engineers.** The ~4 hours/day rule is repeated.

## Notes for the coach
Jones's *"stop going to any of these meetings and see who complains, then put those back on"* aggressive-declutter tactic is caveated as *"only if you're comfortable with your standing"* — read it as a coaching stunt for a coached individual, not a general recommendation. Idiodi's closing coaching-tip-of-the-week: **airplane mode / power button** — the on/off switch of your phone is the most underrated time-management tool in product. Attribute the Eisenhower framing (via a Covey Group workshop early in Jones's career) to Jones; the 7 rules and *meetings-symptom-of-3-culture-problems* framing to Idiodi.
