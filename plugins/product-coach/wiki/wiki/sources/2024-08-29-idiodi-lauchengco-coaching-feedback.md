# Product Therapy Ep 7 — Coaching Feedback

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 7 · SVPG, August 29, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nKYsG8gJpE). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** Lauchengco's teaching of the **SHARED-style three-step feedback frame** ([Radical Candor](https://www.radicalcandor.com/)-adjacent, taught to the Costanoa Ventures CEO cohort): (1) **establish the context** — *"I value our working relationship, and I want to talk about the meeting we just had"*; (2) **share your experience of it** — *"when you said X, it felt like Y — I'm sure that wasn't your intent"*; (3) **ask for the other person's experience** — *"I'm curious what your experience was."* Two Lauchengco phrases that reframe challenge as inquiry: *"I'm curious if…"* and *"have we considered…"*. Positive feedback follows the same rule: **specificity over kudos.** Feedback belongs **in person or Zoom, never email** (Lauchengco's own boss's Friday-email feedback that ruined her weekend). **Email is the worst place on the planet for feedback** — the human channel is what carries intent.

## Notes for the coach
Lauchengco's *"pay it forward"* framing at the end matters: if you've never received good feedback, you won't have anything to give — so the discipline is a durable culture play, not a one-time skill. Idiodi's coaching move: **coaching is not just kudos** — if you never observed *how* the presentation came together, the best you can do is applaud the outcome; if you were involved in the coaching, you can name specifically what the person did that got them there. Attribute the SHARED frame and the "curious if / have we considered" phrases to Lauchengco (crediting her Costanoa 360 experience); attribute *"leaders are not just listening in interviews — they're working as hard as the interviewee"* (a Microsoft interviewing technique) to Lauchengco as well.
