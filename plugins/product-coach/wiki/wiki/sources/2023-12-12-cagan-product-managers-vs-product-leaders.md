# Product Managers vs Product Leaders

> **Citation:** [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · SVPG article · December 12, 2023 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/product-managers-vs-product-leaders/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** Cagan's explicit terminology ruling, prompted by recurring confusion he traced especially to European audiences conflating the two terms: **"product manager" = individual contributor**; **"product leader" = people manager**, responsible for staffing, coaching, developing people, and providing strategic context. He is explicit he didn't coin either term and isn't trying to introduce new ones — "standard is better than better" — he's asking the community to converge on the distinction already implicit in his other writing (*"the product manager is not the boss of anybody"*; *"the single most important responsibility of a product leader is developing the skills of her people"*).

Gives the standard title ladders: **IC track** — associate product manager, product manager, senior product manager, principal product manager. **People-manager track** — manager of product management, director of product management, VP of product, chief product officer. Names one explicit **hybrid title — group product manager (GPM)** — a player-coach role, which Cagan calls out as his favorite path for someone moving from PM into product leadership.
