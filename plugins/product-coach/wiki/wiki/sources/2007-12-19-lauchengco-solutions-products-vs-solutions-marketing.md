# Solutions Products vs. Solutions Marketing

> **Citation:** [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] & [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Solutions Products vs. Solutions Marketing* · SVPG, Dec 19, 2007 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/solutions-products-vs-solutions-marketing/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. **The earliest wiki-attested Lauchengco SVPG article** (2007) — and a rare Lauchengco/Cagan co-authored piece.

## Role
**Primary — a taxonomy piece disambiguating "solutions products" from "solutions marketing"** for enterprise and infrastructure software companies. Notable historically as the earliest wiki-attested Lauchengco SVPG source and a rare co-author with Cagan.

Key material:
- **Motivation.** Many companies stretch the truth to call their product a "platform" or a "solution" — the concepts are important and powerful, and diluting them confuses the market.
- **First: what constitutes a product?** Before defining a solutions product, the article sets the bar for a product itself: people pay for it (or tolerate ads / pay for support); it delivers real distinct value; it works well in multiple customer installations (not custom / special).
- **Solutions products** — actual products that solve a bundled/end-to-end customer problem, not just an integration or configuration story.
- **Solutions marketing** — the go-to-market work of positioning an existing product as *the solution* to a customer problem, even when the product is a component.
