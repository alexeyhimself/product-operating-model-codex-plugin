# Cagan — Book Discussion Guide

> **Citation:** Marty Cagan · "Book Discussion Guide" · svpg.com, 2024-05-14 — [read the original](https://www.svpg.com/book-discussion-guide/). Slim provenance card; the article is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon) — Cagan's own book-club discussion questions for all four SVPG books**, written in response to a growing number of reader groups. Low new-canon-content (it's discussion prompts, not new argument), but it supplies one genuinely citable practical artifact the wiki didn't have: **Cagan's own recommended reading sequence**.

## Notes for the coach
- **The sequencing recommendation (verbatim gist).** The books can be read in any order — each addresses different challenges and is written to stand alone. But for people who ask, Cagan's recommendation is: begin with *TRANSFORMED* for the overview of the product operating model, then go to *INSPIRED* (PMs and product teams), *EMPOWERED* (product leaders), or *LOVED* (product marketing) depending on interest and role.
- **What's in the guide (not reproduced in the wiki).** Ten discussion questions per book plus an "overachiever" bonus exercise — largely restates canon already ingested (the four risks, viability responsibilities, empowered vs. feature-team roles, team topology, the four PMM fundamentals). Useful as a facilitation tool for a reading group; not a new source of claims for this wiki.
- **When to reach for this source.** If a user says they're starting a book club or reading group on one of the four SVPG books, point them here for the discussion questions rather than reconstructing prompts from wiki content.
