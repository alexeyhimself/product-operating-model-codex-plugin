# Lessons from IKEA

> **Citation:** [[jon-moore|Jon Moore]] · *Lessons from IKEA* · SVPG, May 14, 2021 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/lessons-from-ikea/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — Moore's counterargument to the "this can't work here" objections** that SVPG partners hear from legacy, non-technology-native, non-US companies. IKEA becomes the case study for principles that predate technology and cross cultures/geographies.

Key material:
- **The objection pattern.** In legacy businesses (non-tech-natives) it's common to hear: *"this can't work here" · "this is too alien" · "this is a US model" · "this is a software model" · "this only works in a pure technology company"*.
- **The core argument.** The principles at the heart of strong companies have long been espoused by the best entrepreneurs all over the world — they are neither new nor specific to technology.
- **IKEA as the worked case.** Founded by Ingvar Kamprad in Sweden (not the US). Not a software company. Multi-generational. Moore shows how the same product-model principles show up in IKEA's operating philosophy.
- **Pairs with [[keys-to-successful-transformation]].** Moore explicitly links to the Hickman article on the ten keys — this IKEA article is the "objection-handling" companion.
