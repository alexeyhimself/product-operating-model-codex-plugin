## Notes for the coach
- **The term, defined:** a person holding the product-manager title while doing project-management/backlog-administration work, with none of the real product-management skills or responsibilities — *"big hat, no cattle."* Named as *theater* because the title is performed without the substance.
- **The reckoning, and why now.** Cagan had warned for years that delivery-team product owners and feature-team PMs would face a reckoning; he opens this article saying that reckoning has begun, and names generative AI as a likely compounding force (linking back to his earlier "Preparing for the Future").
- **The tell, verbatim:** a popular PM-training program summarizes the role as *"40% communication and coordination, 20% design, 20% engineering, and 20% business"* — Cagan: that's a *project* manager's job description, not a product manager's. Tweak a few words and it's literally a technical-project-manager posting.
- **Not anti-project-manager.** Cagan is explicit a good project manager is valuable — but that role is better covered by a strong tech lead, designer, engineering manager, or program manager than by an inflated "product manager" title. This is also the CEO-cost-cutting angle: theater PMs are the easiest and least costly people to cut, versus a hands-on designer or engineer.
- **The way out, prescribed in order:** an honest skills [[2019-04-08-cagan-coaching-tools-the-assessment|self- or coach-led assessment]] (blind spots are real — get someone who knows what good looks like), then a [[2019-04-22-cagan-coaching-tools-the-plan|plan]] to build the *right* skills. Cagan is explicit the gap is **not** design or engineering (that's what the designer/engineers are for) — it's business skills, data skills, and customer knowledge.
- **Certifications, dismissed.** Cagan restates SVPG's position against PM/PO certifications here: they teach process/methodology, not the product skills and experience that actually matter — the [[2021-06-10-cagan-the-cspo-pathology|CSPO Pathology]] argument restated in miniature.
- **When to cite:** use this article (not the Lenny's podcast) as the origin of the *term itself* and the written statement of the diagnosis; use the Lenny's podcast for the fullest spoken elaboration and the surrounding causal analysis (pandemic hiring, cost of funds, role bloat, SAFe).
