# Product Therapy Ep 4 — Coaching Trust

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[lea-hickman|Lea Hickman]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 4 · SVPG, May 31, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1wuxiOjR9kU). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** Hickman's canonical statement of trust as the foundation of the empowered-team model. The **dimensions** the episode names (Covey/Idiodi framing, elaborated together): **competence trust · character trust · caring trust · communication trust.** Vulnerability is the mechanism — asking for help, admitting *"I don't know, I'll go find out,"* modeling that as a leader before demanding it of the team. **The new-hire trust asymmetry**: on day one the employee arrives with the *highest* trust (they left another job publicly announcing this one); the company arrives with the *lowest* (they don't know what the new hire can actually do). This asymmetry is the origin of Idiodi's onboarding boot camp (see ep 13). Hickman's memorable line on remote work: *"you can't build trust over a document"* — collaborate over conversation, capture in the artifact.

## Notes for the coach
Hickman's positive attribution of the **Idiodi new-employee boot camp** ("I wish I'd had that 25 years ago") pins its origin to Idiodi personally, roughly 8 years before this recording. Also introduces two Hickman coaching moves: (a) *paint a picture bigger than any individual team* (from mala Sharma at Adobe) — the mechanism by which shared vision breaks silo behavior; (b) *hero culture and meeting overhead as symptoms of low trust* — the wiki grounds this on [[trust-over-control]] and links to time-management.
