# Types of Product Coaching

> **Citation:** [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Types of Product Coaching* · SVPG, Feb 22, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/types-of-product-coaching/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the root article for the [[types-of-product-coaching]] concept page.** This is the article Cagan wrote to give the industry a "consistent way of describing the different types of help" — an explicit taxonomy of coaching types that had never been documented before.

Key material:
- Motivation: Cagan announced the experimental *Coach the Coaches* session in London (May 2022), demand exceeded room capacity, and coaches kept asking the same questions about coaching-type distinctions.
- Coaching types are **not mutually exclusive** — many coaches operate at multiple levels, though most have a preference.
- **Delivery coaching** — engineers who help teams stuck at monthly releases move to continuous deployment, put in place instrumentation/telemetry/monitoring, and get the A/B-test infrastructure.
- **Discovery coaching** — the foundation of product coaching; most in-demand type; all successful discovery coaches are former PMs, designers, or tech leads who have learned discovery skills.
- **Product leadership coaching** — coaching product leaders on strategy, staffing, org design.
- **Transformation coaching** — coaching a whole company through the multi-year move to the product model.
- Plus adjacent coaching types (executive coaching, life coaching) that are related but not product coaching.

## Notes for the coach
When a coaching client asks *"what kind of coach do I need?"*, this is the article to cite for the type taxonomy. Cagan explicitly avoids "there's one true way" — a good coach may operate at multiple levels; the taxonomy helps clients articulate what they're asking for. Attribute the type framework itself to Cagan (his own; he says up front the terminology is his but the concepts predate the article — he'd been using them informally for years).
