# Transformation in Action

> **Citation:** [[jon-moore|Jon Moore]] · *Transformation in Action* · SVPG, May 22, 2020 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/transformation-in-action/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. This article was drawn from Moore's chapter in the then-forthcoming *EMPOWERED* (2020).

## Role
**Primary — the SVPG article-form root for The Guardian transformation case.** Moore's own EMPOWERED chapter, published as an SVPG article to answer the "what does product look like in companies that have successfully transformed?" question. **The 2020 SVPG article root for the Guardian case**, complementary to (and predating) the mentions in the 2022 [[2022-04-13-cagan-common-transformation-pitfalls-producttank-oslo|Cagan Oslo talk]] which is currently cited as root for [[guardian-eyewitness]].

Key material:
- **The setup.** June 2007 — Steve Jobs unveils the iPhone. Guardian, under editor Alan Rusbridger, was already the most digitally ambitious newspaper in the world.
- **Moore's account of the transformation** — as an SVPG partner who was there during and after. This is his first-person case study of what an actually-transformed org looks like from the inside.
- Frames the transformation not as "we did all the tools" but as a cultural / operating-model shift that made the eyewitness product possible.
- Directly complements Cagan's later use of the Guardian case as a keynote example.
