# A Better Way

> **Citation:** [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] · *A Better Way* · SVPG, Sep 14, 2018 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/a-better-way/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. Editorial intro by [[marty-cagan|Cagan]] naming Lauchengco as *"the brains behind SVPG"* and *"one of our industry's true thought leaders in marketing, product, and especially product marketing."*

## Role
**Primary — Lauchengco's own origin story**, published for #WomenInProduct as a *"celebration"* piece by Cagan. Contains the *questioning the status quo* thesis (a foundational trait of the PM/PMM craft in Lauchengco's telling) and her Microsoft Word-for-DOS end-of-life packaging story ($1M cost savings from redesigning the packaging of a soon-obsolete product).

Key material:
- **The habit.** Questioning the status quo — inherited from her father, developed through pre-Google-maps teenage life-hacks, embedded through concert-crowd-navigation ("Don't let go").
- **The Microsoft Word-for-DOS story.** As the most junior PM, given the "collection of jobs no one else really wanted" including end-of-life-ing Word-for-DOS. Simplified the packaging design → $1M cost savings from a product she was supposedly just "babysitting."
- **The distinction.** *"Challenging the status quo does not mean challenging reason, facts themselves or doing so merely for the sake of being contrarian. It means taking as a given facts and constraints as they are and pushing for a better, smarter way through them."*
- **The role in innovation.** Creation is essential for true innovation; questioning-the-status-quo is foundational to creation.
