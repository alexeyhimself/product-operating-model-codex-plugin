# Market Fit

> **Citation:** [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] · *Market Fit* · SVPG, Aug 17, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/market-fit/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. Article-form companion to [[loved|LOVED]] Ch 11.

## Role
**Primary — the SVPG article-form root for the [[market-fit]] concept page.** Currently [[market-fit]] cites LOVED Ch 11 as primary; this article is Lauchengco's SVPG-article version of the same material, published as the book launched.

Key material:
- **The "market fit" definition.** *"Discovering 'market pull.' What makes a customer need or want your product enough that they take immediate action to learn more, try, or buy? And what makes this pattern repeatable?"*
- **Market fit vs product-market fit.** Product-market fit is the composite; market fit is the *market side* — often underdeveloped, more discovered *in parallel* with product discovery than independently.
- **Beyond usability, feasibility, viability.** Value risk = the discovery work about *market fit*. Most difficult and most important, yet most underdeveloped.
- **The "people say they will" trap.** People say they'll buy a lot of things. Tests differ in placing decisions/actions in realistic market context.
- **Beyond "would you buy this."** Assessing market fit requires probing market conditions that motivate action or create urgency — distribution, messaging, word-of-mouth, comparison sites, Reddit.
- **PMM partnership.** Good PMs partner with product marketing early — PMM can apply more market nuance to discovery, can catch segmentation opportunities the PM might miss.
