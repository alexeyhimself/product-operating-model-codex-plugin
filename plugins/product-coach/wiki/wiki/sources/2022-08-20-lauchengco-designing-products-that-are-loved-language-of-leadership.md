# Lauchengco — Designing Products that are LOVED (Language of Leadership)

> **Citation:** Martina Lauchengco (SVPG / Costanoa Ventures) interviewed by Mike Hauge, *Language of Leadership* #021 · YouTube, published 2022-08-20 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nxIS35sggDw). Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon) — the conceptual-distinctions episode.** Least overlap with the two 2022 talks: no keystroke study, no canvas walkthrough. Instead an interview built around the *tensions* in *LOVED*, plus the clearest published statement of Lauchengco's **positioning ≠ messaging** distinction.

## What it backs
- concepts: [[positioning]] (**the distinction, sharply drawn**: positioning used to be a *statement* — in an era of ten companies and one store, what you said was the position. Today, with millions of apps and any single software category as crowded as the planet has cities, your position is *the sum of everything you do* — who you affiliate with, when you show up, what you sponsor, what you bundle at what price — not only what you say. Her YouTube TV worked example: the package, the price, the sports-adjacency, the timing, all of it *is* the positioning), [[messaging]] (**against the Mad Libs template** — "our [blank] built for [blank] is the best [blank] in the [blank] market": a fine thinking exercise, *"terrible at generating usable messaging"*, because it kitchen-sinks everything and then everything feels obliged to stay together. Prioritise what the customer has the capacity to hear *now*; the rest is the second conversation or a page deeper), [[pmm-pm-partnership]], [[product-marketing-by-stage]], [[product-marketing]]
- competencies: [[product-leadership]], [[leading-product-marketing]]
- frameworks: [[partnering-with-executives]], [[one-sheet-messaging-canvas]]

## Notes for the coach
- **The say-do gap.** Lauchengco's term (she teaches it to her Berkeley students) for the distance between what people say they believe and what they actually do — bias toward observed action, product data, clicks. **But** she immediately qualifies it, and the qualification is the useful part: revealed preference tells you which of the options you *showed* was preferred, not which option is right. If C wins but C doesn't move you toward the position you need in 6–12 months, ship a variation of C and go do the market-education work. Backs [[positioning]] and [[product-discovery]]; a good counterweight to naive data-driven prioritisation.
- **Go-to-market shapes the product, not just the other way round.** Product-led vs. top-down direct sales produce genuinely different products (ease of use, shareability). Her Costanoa example — an identity company whose messaging iteration ("just-in-time access to the most important things, in the right context, at the right time") *changed what they built and emphasised* — is the concrete version of the claim in [[2022-05-27-lauchengco-rethink-marketing-for-tech-products-red-id]] that positioning work reshapes roadmaps.
- **Positional vs. relational authority.** Her own leadership arc — from her father's Army-shaped model of positional authority to influence without it — is the frame the interview hangs on, and is the same problem a product marketer faces structurally: cross-functional influence with no line authority over anyone. Useful for [[product-leadership]] and [[coaching-up]]; biographical, not model canon.
