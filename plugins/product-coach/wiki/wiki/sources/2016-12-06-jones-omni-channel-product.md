# Omni-Channel Product

> **Citation:** [[chris-jones|Chris Jones]] · *Omni-Channel Product* · SVPG, Dec 6, 2016 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/omni-channel-product/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the SVPG article naming the "Legacy PM vs Digital PM" pattern** in omni-channel businesses (banking, insurance, transportation, media, retail — companies that had core value before the internet and now have digital + physical touchpoints).

Key material:
- **The pattern.** Legacy PM (actuarial models, credit products, financial derivatives, academic data sets) vs. Digital PM (e-commerce, app, digital experience). Often strong org/cultural separation; sometimes they don't talk at all.
- **The consequence.** Fragmented customer experiences (airline notifications ahead of the ground crew; unclear whether to call web or phone support at your bank; different in-store vs online product catalogs).
- **The innovation blocker.** Product organization can't innovate around a holistic value view (Jones's *pay-per-mile auto insurance* example — requires intertwining actuarial risk models with digital usage data).
- **Three fixes:** (a) ensure Digital PMs deeply understand core business value Legacy PMs create; (b) embed Legacy PMs into cross-functional teams — ideally co-located with a Digital PM, designer, engineers during discovery; (c) remember discovery is about reducing risk — value, usability, feasibility — regardless of Legacy or Digital origin.
- **The customer insight.** *"Customers don't distinguish between the Legacy and Digital aspects of products."*

## Notes for the coach
When a retail / insurance / bank / airline / media client asks about a Legacy-vs-Digital-PM split, cite this article's diagnosis and its three fixes. Attribute the "Legacy PM vs Digital PM" terminology to Jones (2016). The fix is not to consolidate the roles — different skill sets are real — but to embed and cross-train.
