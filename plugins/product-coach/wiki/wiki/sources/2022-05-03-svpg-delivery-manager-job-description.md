# SVPG — Delivery Manager Job Description

> **Citation:** SVPG · "Delivery Manager Job Description" · svpg.com, 2022-05-03 — [read the original](https://www.svpg.com/delivery-manager-job-description/). Slim provenance card; the article is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** SVPG's **template job description** for the [[delivery-manager]] role — the operational form of the mindset described in [[2014-07-13-cagan-the-delivery-manager-role|The Delivery Manager Role]] (2014). The JD's own opening italics make the distinction Cagan cares about most: *"A classic program/project manager often behaves as if their role is about executing on a top-down, command-and-control plan, ensuring compliance with the process, usually undermining empowerment in the process. In contrast, a delivery manager is there to remove impediments for the product team. Very much in a servant-leadership manner."*

## What it backs
- competencies: [[delivery-manager]] — the article-form of the accountabilities the wiki carries. Specifically:
  - **Multi-team scope** by default: *"A Delivery Manager supports multiple cross-functional product teams (squads), and serves as the Scrum Master for each of those product teams."*
  - **Key responsibilities** (verbatim): impediment identification/removal, cross-team/squad dependency tracking, [[high-integrity-commitments|high-integrity commitment]] tracking, proactive visibility, meeting minimization + quality, team-driven decision culture, coaching **only where appropriate** (*"coaching is the primary responsibility of teams' managers"*).
  - **Basic qualifications**: general understanding of large-scale software systems, delivery & go-to-market methods and metrics, general understanding of PM / designer / engineer / QA / DevOps / product-marketer roles, 2+ years in engineering with common Agile methods (Scrum/Kanban/XP), **Certified Scrum Master (CSM)**.
- frameworks: [[high-integrity-commitments]] — the JD names *"Management and tracking of all high-integrity commitments"* as a responsibility. Corroborates [[delivery-manager]]'s accountability list.
- concepts: [[product-delivery]] — the delivery-manager role sits inside the delivery process, not inside product judgment.
- entities: [[svpg]], [[marty-cagan]].

## Notes for the coach
- **CSM is a "basic qualification," not a certification requirement Cagan disparages elsewhere.** This is worth flagging: the [[2021-06-10-cagan-the-cspo-pathology|CSPO pathology]] article criticizes Certified Scrum Product Owner training as a false path *for PMs*. The **CSM (Certified Scrum Master) for delivery managers** is a different case — SVPG's own JD names it as a basic qualification, and Cagan does not treat it as a pathology. Do not conflate the two certifications.
- **"Coaching is the primary responsibility of teams' managers"** — the JD names coaching as *outside* the delivery manager's primary lane. Delivery managers may coach *where appropriate* but the coaching mandate lives with the PM/designer/engineering managers. Matches [[product-leadership]] Ch 3.
- **Servant leadership** is called out in the JD's opening italics — quote directly when a company frames the role as "the person who tells the team what to do."
- **Multi-team scaling.** The JD's *"supports multiple cross-functional product teams (squads)"* framing corroborates the [[delivery-manager]] page's "scale dependence" note — a single DM covering several teams is the SVPG default, not one DM per team.
- **Diversity clause.** The JD carries SVPG's standard closing paragraph on diverse perspectives, matching the PM/Director JDs in the same set — the three JDs form a consistent hiring frame.
