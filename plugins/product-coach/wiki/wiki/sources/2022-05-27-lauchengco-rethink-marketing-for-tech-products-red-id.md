# Lauchengco — Rethink Marketing for Tech Products (RED-ID)

> **Citation:** Martina Lauchengco (SVPG), *Rethink marketing for tech products*, RED-ID meetup (Tel Aviv) · YouTube, published 2022-05-27 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmBWznsc63k). Same four-fundamentals talk as [[2022-04-27-lauchengco-loved-lean-product-meetup]], one month later, with a **substantially different and more valuable Q&A**. Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon) — cite this one for the Q&A, the other for the talk.** The prepared content overlaps heavily (Word keystroke study; Workiva multi-screen; Looker vs RJ Metrics; go-to-market canvas; Splunk Trust). What is unique here is ~15 minutes of live questions on **where product marketing should report, how PM and PMM actually split the work, and what to do when marketing says "we can't sell this roadmap."**

## What it backs
- competencies: [[leading-product-marketing]] (**the reporting-line answer, and it is not dogma:** put product marketing wherever a leader knows how to make it successful. Most companies put it under marketing; if that leader treats it as launch-manager and collateral-producer, put it under product instead — *"put it wherever it can be a full-powered force,"* and where it will carry political capital), [[product-marketing]]
- concepts: [[pmm-pm-partnership]] (**the yin-and-yang description**: both sides read the same incoming signal, one asking *what should we build*, the other *what should our market actions be*; weekly cadence minimum. Plus the strongest claim in the source: **the product team is the party with the most power to make a mediocre product marketer into a real partner**, by investing in their product depth), [[product-marketing-by-stage]] (early-stage: someone wears the hat whether or not they hold the title; mature: PMM specialises by market/vertical/segment and becomes less dynamic on the product side), [[pmm-ambassador]], [[pmm-strategist]], [[pmm-storyteller]], [[pmm-evangelist]], [[messaging]], [[positioning]]
- frameworks: [[one-sheet-gtm-canvas]] (**the when → why → what → how inversion** stated most clearly here — most teams run *what → how → why → when*; inverting it is what puts market wind at your back. The Peloton / Harry Styles example: the same instructor, released against an album drop, drew ~110k participants in five days), [[product-marketing-metrics]], [[agile-product-marketing]]
- entities: [[martina-lauchengco]], [[loved]], [[workiva]]

## Notes for the coach
- **The "we can't sell that roadmap" question** gets a real answer: product marketing should have a seat at product planning, whatever the cadence, and the canvas is the tool for surfacing the disconnect *before* the roadmap is committed. Lauchengco has never seen a team do the canvas's top two rows — outside world, then product milestones — without changing something about their roadmap.
- **The Chris Jones anecdote**, told on herself: her husband ([[chris-jones|Jones]], SVPG partner) would say a release date was set because *the work was done*, and she would answer that "done" is never a reason — where is the market thinking? A rare, disarming statement of how unnatural market-timing is even for excellent product people.
- **"It's not what you want to say, it's what they need to hear"** is her one-line test for messaging, and she pairs it with a practical instruction for product people: you already run discovery tests, so **test messaging inside them** — ask a participant to describe what they just saw to a colleague. Cheapest possible messaging validation; backs [[messaging]] and [[one-sheet-messaging-canvas]].
- **Splunk Trust** (fezzes, capes, unabashed embrace of the deepest product nerds) is her scaled-evangelism example: you cannot educate a technical market yourself, so reward the people who will. Backs [[product-evangelism]] and [[pmm-evangelist]].
