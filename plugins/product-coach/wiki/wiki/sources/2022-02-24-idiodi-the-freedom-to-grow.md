# The Freedom to Grow

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] · *The Freedom to Grow* · SVPG, Feb 24, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/the-freedom-to-grow/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — Idiodi's Datasite transformation article featuring [[thomas-fredell|Thomas Fredell]] (Chief Product Officer)** and the vivid metaphor of "room after room without a ray of light, every one filled with ten-foot-high cubicles." Sibling to [[2023-06-02-idiodi-breaking-down-walls|Breaking Down Walls]] (Jeremiah Ivan, VP Eng) and [[2022-02-24-idiodi-failure-as-a-springboard-to-success|Failure as a Springboard]].

Key material:
- **Fredell's metaphor.** *"Imagine room after room without a ray of light, every one of those rooms filled with ten-foot-high cubicles, every one of those cubicles an impenetrable wall totally averse to any form of collaboration or shared vision."*
- **The rebrand.** Merrill Corporation → Datasite (2020). Reflected the completion of the 2018 digital transformation and divestiture of the legacy financial printing business.
- **The CEO vision.** Fast-moving, decentralized business; people and ideas take precedence over the status quo.
- **The culture change.** From slow-moving, hierarchical, risk-averse → collaborative, fast, learning-first.
