# Product Therapy Ep 3 — Coaching Product Sense

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 3 · SVPG, May 2, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvemAAB0ies). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** The definitive Cagan-and-Idiodi statement of *product sense as earned, not innate.* Cagan's history is the crux: he avoided the term for years because people used it as an excuse (*"you either have it or you don't"*); he used *"do your homework"* instead, and eventually adopted the phrase only after explicitly redefining it. The four foundations of onboarding-to-product-sense: **users and customers · data (qualitative + quantitative) · the company (constraints, personalities, GTM, finance) · the competitive/technology landscape.** Introduces the **three traits that either enable or block product sense**: **humility · curiosity · empathy** (agency is prerequisite; see [[coaching-agency]]). Idiodi's vulnerable career story — winning an innovation competition at 23, losing $26M across 17 failures, rebuilding through immersion — grounds the "you can develop it" claim.

## Notes for the coach
Two additional teaching moves worth surfacing: (a) **the "smartest person at the company" onboarding technique** (Idiodi) — publicly attach yourself to a domain expert in visible conference rooms, so the company sees both your humility and their endorsement; (b) **biographies for decision-making** — Idiodi's former CEO who insisted on Lincoln, Steve Jobs, Tony Fadell (*Build*) — not for facts but for how great leaders *made critical decisions* under uncertainty. Domain expertise is a **double-edged sword**: too much can crowd out the humility and curiosity that keep product sense sharp (Shreyas Doshi's *dogma-vs-expertise* frame is invoked, though not named).
