# Product Therapy Ep 12 — Coaching Founder-Style Leadership

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 12 · SVPG, January 9, 2025 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RBmkvJZLv_s). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the fullest podcast-form statement of the [[founder-style-leadership]] concept.** Cagan and Idiodi elaborate on the wiki's existing founder-style-leadership grounding (from *[[2025-03-03-cagan-why-your-product-operating-model-is-broken|Why Your Product Operating Model is Broken]]*, Feb 2025) with these additions: (a) **it's not a "mode"** — you don't turn it on and off — it's a *leadership style*, i.e. how the leader continuously operates; (b) **"better management, not less management"** as the tagline for what it corrects; (c) **the two ingredients at scale = product sense + coaching**, mapping cleanly onto Amazon's *"dive deep"* + *"hire and develop the best"*, Apple's *"experts lead experts"* + *"deep expertise"*, and Google's *"substance first"* + *"develop others through coaching"*; (d) the **Mike Fisher / MyFitnessPal** example of an executive who *"crafted the questions"* for a 1:1 — the crisp coaching move that separates founder-style from either dictation or delegation; (e) explicit critique of the **European "people-only manager"** pattern where coaching means *"I don't have to know anything about the subject."* Closes with **pilot-team** as the practical mechanism for shifting an org toward this style.

## Notes for the coach
Cagan's 1984 Steve Jobs quote — *"we went out and hired a bunch of professional management… they knew how to manage but they didn't know how to do anything"* — is the wiki's canonical 1984 attestation that "professional management" as a scaling default doesn't work in tech-powered companies. The **Bill Campbell** *"you cannot be a good manager without being a good coach"* line is re-affirmed here (fuller grounding on [[bill-campbell]] and [[coaching-mindset]]). Attribute the *"founder-*style*"* renaming (versus Paul Graham's *"founder mode"*) to Cagan, and the *"where's the line between founder-style and micromanagement?"* coaching question to Idiodi.
