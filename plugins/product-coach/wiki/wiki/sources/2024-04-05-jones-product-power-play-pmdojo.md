# Jones — Product Power Play (PMDojo, Bosky Mukherjee)

> **Citation:** Chris Jones (SVPG) interviewed by Bosky Mukherjee, *PMDojo's Product Power Play: How to make product innovation a habit* · LinkedIn Live / YouTube, published 2024-04-05 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgwoa7K4U3c). Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon)** — the most *career-shaped* of the four Jones interviews. Lighter on model mechanics, heavier on what an individual can do inside a company that isn't there yet.

## What it backs
- frameworks: [[product-career-development]] (Jones on luck and the limits of career engineering; ~four years as his own stay-in-a-job number; "put 90% into the job you have, 10% into the next"; leave when you stop learning *and* can't see a path to fixing it), [[coaching-the-pm]]
- concepts: [[imposter-syndrome]] (Jones: fear and anxiety drove much of his early career; everybody has it, *"especially"* the most confident-seeming person in the room), [[empowered-product-teams]], [[strategic-context]], [[pmm-pm-partnership]]
- competencies: [[product-manager]] (the two things to raise your game on: **know your customers better than anyone in the company**, and **know how the business actually works** — how money is made, what sales does, why legal says no), [[product-leadership]] (a first-line manager should be spending at least ~50% of their time coaching), [[leading-product-marketing]]
- entities: [[chris-jones]], [[christian-idiodi]]

## Notes for the coach
- **"What would you do?" not "what should I do?"** — Jones's own reframing device for asking for help without handing over the decision. You keep the agency and stay allowed to be vulnerable. Small, portable, and a good coaching-conversation tool; backs [[coaching-up]] and [[decision-making]].
- **Go-to-market is part of product, and Jones learned it the hard way.** His Vontu story: how the product was *sold* had massive implications for how the experience had to be architected. His practical device — have every PM sponsor at least one **live deal** at any time, while guarding against over-indexing on that one customer. Backs [[pmm-sales-partnership]] and [[reference-customer-program]].
- **"Your stakeholder is not your customer."** Named as the phrase that makes SVPG crazy: your *company's* customers are your customers. Serve the customer in ways that work for the business — Jones calls the difference "word play" that turns out to be profoundly different in practice.
- **Second independent airing of Idiodi's "if you know, you will care"** — here Jones describes hearing it on a podcast and being struck by it as a reframe of the pressure to *have opinions*: your job isn't to conjure opinions, it's to go learn, and the opinions arrive. See [[2024-11-02-jones-from-features-to-outcomes-khodor]] and [[2023-12-21-idiodi-essence-of-product-management-lennys]].
- **Innovation is not on-demand.** *"You can't be innovative on demand… you put this group together and you give them a problem and you hold them accountable to finding a solution, and innovation happens."* The cleanest one-line statement in the corpus of why the model is an innovation mechanism rather than a process.
