# Principles over Process

> **Citation:** [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Principles over Process* · SVPG, Jan 16, 2023 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/principles-over-process/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the eponymous root article for the [[principles-over-process]] principle**, which until now was grounded only on *[[transformed|TRANSFORMED]]* plus adjacent articles. Short (≈700 words) and framing rather than expository: it is a pointer to a talk plus a statement of the thesis.

Key material:
- **The occasion.** [Crisp](http://www.crisp.se/) invited Cagan to a virtual conference of deliberately clashing viewpoints, before an audience of **mainly Agile coaches**. He notes the organizers' credit in wanting their audience to hear from "people outside of the traditional Agile evangelists," and gives fair warning: *"if you're an Agile coach, especially if you make your living trying to implement SAFe, large sections of this talk will be more than a little uncomfortable."*
- **The companion video.** Crisp published the recording (≈1 hour): [youtube.com/watch?v=mcnxxdzC2fY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mcnxxdzC2fY) — *not separately carded; it is the artifact this article exists to point at.*
- **The thesis.** *"There are the Agile principles, as captured in the manifesto, and there are the many Agile processes created ostensibly around those principles… process people essentially took over Agile, and today most companies follow the processes, but aren't even close to living the principles."*
- **Generalized beyond Agile.** *"This phenomenon is not unique to Agile. There is a long history in our industry of process people taking over otherwise good principles, and either applying them where they don't make any sense, or being so dogmatic that the teams lose sight of what matters."*
- **Provenance value — this is where the first-principles series is announced.** *"I intend to write a series of articles diving into the **product first principles** that I briefly describe in the video."* Written Jan 2023, a year before *TRANSFORMED* (2024) formalized the 20 first principles. This article is the **public origin point of the first-principles project** that the wiki's [[overview]] is built on.

## Notes for the coach
Two distinct uses. First, as the **cleanest one-line statement of the principle**: follow the principles, not the rituals. Second — and more useful when a coachee is an Agile coach or works with one — as evidence that Cagan **sought out that audience rather than talking past it**, and framed the argument as "what went wrong with Agile" rather than "Agile is wrong." The article is notably respectful of the Agile *manifesto* while scathing about the *processes* built atop it; that distinction is the whole argument and is easy to lose when the principle is quoted as a slogan.

The dating is the other reason to keep this card: when asked where the 20 first principles came from, this is the earliest public statement of intent to write them.
