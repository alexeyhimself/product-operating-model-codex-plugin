# Hickman — Transforming to a Product Culture (Mind the Product London 2017)

> **Citation:** Lea Hickman (SVPG), *Transforming to a Product Culture*, keynote at Mind the Product London 2017; write-up by James Gadsby Peet, published on Mind the Product 2017-11-24 — [read](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/transforming-product-culture-lea-hickman/). Slim provenance card; the write-up is **not reproduced**. The write-up is the only linkable public artifact of the talk, and is treated as the primary record for citation purposes.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** Hickman's **earliest wiki-linked long-form talk** — pre-*TRANSFORMED*, pre–*Product Therapy* — and the single tightest short-form statement of the *product-culture* argument she later builds out across the [[transformation]] canon. Predates every other Hickman card in `wiki/sources/` by 6+ years and gives her the same *culture-first* framing on the record long before the phrase *transformation theater* was coined ([[2024-02-27-hickman-transformation-theater|Feb 2024]]).

## What it backs
- concepts: [[product-culture]] (**Hickman's compact definition**: *"product culture is more than the sum of its parts — a combination of the right people, the right process, all focused on the right outcomes"*), [[outcomes-over-output]] (**the critical shift** Hickman puts at the center of a product-culture transformation: *"directing product teams with tasks → making the team investigate solutions to goals"*; her formulation — *"no organisation can establish a strong product culture without making this change"* — is more categorical than most of the SVPG canon), [[trust-over-control]] (credibility as the currency between product teams and the rest of the org)
- competencies: [[product-manager]] (**the credibility argument**: autonomy is *earned* through execution + accountability + integrity — *"do what you say you're going to do"*; and **transparency in both directions** — *"be just as comfortable delivering bad news as positive results, otherwise you only tell half the story and people won't believe you appreciate the whole situation"*)
- frameworks: [[hiring-and-onboarding]] (**Hickman's three-trait bar for hiring PMs**, hired for personality and soft skills ahead of technical skills — *"you can learn a skill, but some of the factors that make a good product manager are inherent within a personality"*):
  1. **Intellectual curiosity** — beyond academia and content, curiosity about *yourself*.
  2. **Natural collaboration** — *"if you are more concerned about being right than being open, you will struggle to be a product manager."*
  3. **Grit** — passion and perseverance; without it *"you'll be soon lost and moving on."*
- diagnostics: [[stakeholder-relationship-health]] (**the rules-of-engagement fix** for chronic business-vs-product-goals tension — collaborate with stakeholders to define what's acceptable and what's not for everybody, establish these rules *early*, and *"constantly revisit and iterate them, as with anything good product people do"*; a small amount of tension can be healthy, *"but if it's constant and not the exception then it ends up becoming toxic"*)
- concepts: [[transformation]] (the executive-team ↔ product-team disconnect as the *most obvious and most debilitating* form the disconnect takes — Hickman's 2017 diagnosis of what a transformation is actually up against)
- entities: [[lea-hickman]] (her earliest ingested SVPG-era public talk; establishes her *culture-first* transformation stance six years before [[2024-02-27-hickman-transformation-theater|*Transformation Theater*]] and seven before her *TRANSFORMED* co-authorship)

## Notes for the coach
- **The three-trait PM hiring bar (curiosity · natural collaboration · grit)** is Hickman's public 2017 formulation and is the earliest wiki-attested version of the "hire for personality first" line the SVPG canon later restates in many forms. Cite from here on [[hiring-and-onboarding]] alongside Cagan's hire-for-potential rule — it is Hickman's operational filter for what "potential" looks like at the interview.
- **"Do what you say you're going to do"** as the earnable-credibility rule. Points forward to [[high-integrity-commitments|high-integrity commitments]] as the mature formulation of the same principle: reliability is the currency; keep the commitment or renegotiate honestly.
- **Business-goals-vs-product-goals tension** and **rules of engagement** — the fix Hickman recommends here (collaboratively define what's acceptable with stakeholders; iterate the rules) is the 2017 shape of what Batch 3's Ep 8 card ([[2024-09-26-idiodi-hickman-coaching-stakeholder-collaboration]]) later formalises as **voice vs. vote** on [[stakeholder-relationship-health]]. Same authorial voice, same premise; the older card is where the *bad-outcomes-if-not-fixed* framing is starkest ("toxic if not the exception").
- **The output→outcomes shift** stated categorically. This card is worth citing whenever a coach is asked whether an org can call itself product-culture-strong while still operating output-based — Hickman's answer, 2017: no.
