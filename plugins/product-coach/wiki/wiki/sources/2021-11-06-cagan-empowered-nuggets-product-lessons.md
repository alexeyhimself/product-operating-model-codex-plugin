# Cagan — The Best Nuggets from EMPOWERED (Product Lessons)

> **Citation:** Marty Cagan on the *Product Lessons* podcast with Ravi · YouTube, published November 6, 2021 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3zdsULCAuBA). His second appearance on the show, three years after the first. Slim provenance card; the transcript is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon) — reinforcement.** An [[empowered|EMPOWERED]]-era Q&A working through the book's claims with audience questions. It adds no doctrine the book does not carry; the one passage worth citing on its own is the **sales-as-customer-proxy rebuttal**, which is crisper here than in the book.

Key material:

(a) **The disconnection diagnosis.** In poor tech companies the technology teams are disconnected from real customers — *"in fact they are encouraged to think of the stakeholders as their customers."*

(b) **The sales-as-proxy-for-the-customer rebuttal, in three moves.** Asked how to handle sales claiming to know customers best because they talk to them constantly:
1. **Different people.** *"Sales talks to **buyers** — that doesn't mean they talk to **users**. Very often they don't talk to users. And realize sales also talks to **prospects** more than customers."*
2. **Not irrelevant, just partial.** *"It's a pretty useful dimension for sure, so it's not irrelevant — but it is just one piece of a much bigger puzzle."*
3. **It is a symptom, not a fact about sales.** *"If what you just said is true at your company, first of all it tells me **product is not doing what they need to do**."*

(c) **The cadence that resolves it, with the effort estimate.** *"The product team should be the ones talking to customers more than anybody — more than sales. And that's not that hard actually; it doesn't take all that much time. Normally product teams are talking to customers **every single week**, and usually it's several customers every single week"* — across different kinds of customer. Consistent with [[continuous-interviewing]] and, five years before Torres's Ep-44 finding, the same weekly bar.

## Notes for the coach
- Use (b) verbatim when a sales leader claims proxy authority over product decisions. The third move is the important one and the one most people skip: if sales genuinely knows the customer better than the product team does, that is a **product** failure to fix, not a sales overreach to argue about.
- Otherwise reinforcement — attribute EMPOWERED's claims to [[empowered|the book]], not to this episode.
