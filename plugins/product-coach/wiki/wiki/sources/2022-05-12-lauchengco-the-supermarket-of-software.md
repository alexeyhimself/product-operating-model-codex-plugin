# The Supermarket of Software

> **Citation:** [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] · *The Supermarket of Software* · SVPG, May 12, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/the-supermarket-of-software/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. LOVED-era article.

## Role
**Primary — Lauchengco's counter to the "if we build it, they will come" myth** and the SVPG canonical statement that GTM must be built *as you build the product*, not layered on top afterwards.

Key material:
- **The supermarket metaphor.** A physical supermarket has 40k–50k products; software is 100× more. Most of what a shopper sees they don't know.
- **The martech proof.** ~9,932 martech solutions in 2022 — nearly as many as cities on the planet. 5.5M apps across iOS + Android — the population of Miami.
- **The GTM-as-you-build discipline.** Product marketing talent is finite; PMs must think GTM *as they build*, not hand it over to PMM at launch.
- **Value must displace an incumbent.** *"For any product to reach its adoption and commercial potential, its value must be exceptional enough to displace an existing product, behavior or belief."*
- **The six-areas-of-value trap.** You might have six areas of product value but message only one. Not because the others don't add value, but because *messaging all six diminishes the most convincing area*. Message convergence, not comprehensiveness.
