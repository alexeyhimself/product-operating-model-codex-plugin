# Product Therapy Ep 8 — Coaching Stakeholder Collaboration

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[lea-hickman|Lea Hickman]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 8 · SVPG, September 26, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U9m-CdIQ_cE). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** The cleanest wiki definition of a **stakeholder** yet: *someone with a **vote**, not just a **voice**.* A vote = **veto power**: they can say no and the product doesn't ship (CEO, chief legal, head of sales, CFO in relevant scopes). A voice = influence without a vote — worth hearing, but does not multiply into everyone-is-a-stakeholder. Hickman's Merrill acquisition example: PCI-compliance looked like a *stop sign* (legal veto), turned out to be a *yield sign* (two-year window to become compliant) — the point being that a good PM investigates the constraint, doesn't just accept the label. **Enablers, not barriers**: the Chinese-launch counter-story where the head of legal wanted to *sue the government* to enter the market. Two coaching moves elevated to canon here: (a) **collaborate over prototypes, not artifacts** — *"you can't build trust over a document"*; (b) **one-on-ones, not group meetings** — because group meetings degenerate into design-by-committee, HIPPO, and the "Star Wars syndrome" of departments trading questions across silos. Closes with **the Rule of Improv** (*"yes, and…"*) as the stakeholder-idea-handling default.

## Notes for the coach
Idiodi's *"the best way to know if it's legal in your company is if legal built it with you"* line generalizes the point: partner-with-stakeholders is not a soft skill, it is *how* viability actually gets solved. Hickman's *"stakeholder-driven roadmap"* framing here connects to [[stakeholder-relationship-health|Level-1 stakeholder health]]. The **five-Whys / Rule of Improv mashup** at the close is Hickman's practical script for turning a stakeholder feature-request into a discovery conversation without shutting the person down.
