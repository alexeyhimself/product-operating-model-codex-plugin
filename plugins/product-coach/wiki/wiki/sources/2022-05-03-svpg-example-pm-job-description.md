# SVPG — Example PM Job Description

> **Citation:** SVPG · "Example PM Job Description" · svpg.com, 2022-05-03 — [read the original](https://www.svpg.com/example-pm-job-description/). Slim provenance card; the article is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** SVPG's **template PM job description** for a company built on empowered product teams — the operational, hiring-and-onboarding form of what [[product-manager]] specifies conceptually. Companion to [[2022-05-03-svpg-product-director-job-description|Product Director JD]] and [[2022-05-03-cagan-the-vp-product-role|The VP Product Role]] — the three-tier SVPG JD set.

## What it backs
- competencies: [[product-manager]] — the article-form of the accountabilities the wiki carries:
  - **Value + viability, in the JD's own words.** *"While the product designer is accountable for ensuring the solution is usable, and the engineers are accountable for ensuring the solution is feasible, the product manager is accountable for ensuring the solution is both valuable and viable."*
  - The four business-context areas the PM must contribute: *"marketing, sales, service, finance, legal, and privacy."* Plus **deep customer/data knowledge**, and **industry-trend + competitive-landscape tracking**.
  - **PM is an IC role**, not a manager: *"This job, as with the other roles on the product team, is an individual contributor role. You will need to be able to influence your teammates, as well as colleagues, stakeholders, and key executives, through your use of data and logic."*
- concepts: [[the-four-big-risks]] — the JD explicitly enumerates *valuable, viable, usable, feasible* and assigns each to a trio member. This is the four-risks framework in JD form.
- concepts: [[empowered-product-teams]] — the JD's opening frame: *"We empower our product teams to solve hard problems – customer problems and business problems – in ways that our customers love, yet work for our business."*
- principles: [[outcomes-over-output]] — *"Shipping is necessary, but not sufficient. We look for product managers that are not afraid of signing up for results, even when this means they have to work through others to achieve the necessary results."*
- concepts: [[the-product-team-trio]] — cross-functional teams *"generally comprised of a product manager, a product designer, and several engineers."*
- entities: [[svpg]], [[marty-cagan]].

## Notes for the coach
- **The domain-experience question is explicit.** The JD opens with a note: *"there's no requirement here for specific domain knowledge, and that's intentional, and not just an effort to keep this job description generic"* — linking out to Cagan's [role-of-domain-experience](https://svpg.com/the-role-of-domain-experience/) article. When a hiring manager tries to add a hard domain requirement, this is the counterargument.
- **Necessary qualifications the JD names** (verbatim structure):
  - Bachelor's degree *or equivalent practical experience.*
  - Demonstrated understanding of **modern [[product-discovery|product discovery]] and [[product-delivery|product delivery]]** techniques.
  - 2 years working on tech-powered products — as PM, designer, engineer, data analyst, data scientist, or user researcher. **The adjacent-role paths are named in the JD**; this is where the wiki's "best PMs often come from engineering" claim gets a hiring-side anchor.
  - Demonstrated ability to **learn multiple functional areas** — engineering, design, finance, sales, marketing.
  - **Written narrative** ability: *"lay out your argument in a well-structured, data-informed, written narrative"* — the Amazon-style written-narrative discipline codified into the JD.
- **What the JD deliberately omits.** No CSPO / Scrum-certification requirement. No "years as a PM" hard minimum. No hard domain. This is deliberate — see the [[2021-06-10-cagan-the-cspo-pathology|CSPO pathology]] argument for why the certification would be an anti-signal here.
- **How to use this.** Recommend as a starting template for any company standing up an empowered-team PM hire. The three-tier set with [[2022-05-03-svpg-product-director-job-description|the director JD]] and [[2022-05-03-cagan-the-vp-product-role|the VP JD]] gives a consistent hiring frame from IC through senior leadership.
