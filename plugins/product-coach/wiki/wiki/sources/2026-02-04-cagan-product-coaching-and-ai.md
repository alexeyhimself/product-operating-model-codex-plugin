# Cagan — Product Coaching and AI

> **Citation:** Marty Cagan · "Product Coaching and AI" · svpg.com, 2026-02-04 — [read the original](https://www.svpg.com/product-coaching-and-ai/). Slim provenance card; the article is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon) — the ROOT article for [[model-as-product-coach|model-as-product-coach]].** Cagan's own words: *"This article represents a significant change to what we have been advocating for the past two decades."* The written articulation of SVPG's official position shift after 20 years of arguing that coaching must come from your manager. It names both the diagnosis (why the previous model has failed) and the prescription (use a foundation model, configured with project files + project instructions + strategic context). The April 2026 [[2026-04-14-cagan-ai-product-coaching-women-in-product|Women-in-Product interview]] is its spoken how-to companion.

**This wiki is one implementation of the prescription** — the project files are the [`wiki/`](../) body, the project instructions are [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md); the third layer (the org's strategic-context baseline) is supplied separately.

## Notes for the coach — organized by article section

### Opening (§ the reckoning + AI-as-theater)
- **Cagan's own words on the shift:** *"This article represents a significant change to what we have been advocating for the past two decades."*
- **The AI-as-theater warning.** *"For many people, their use of AI is simply exposing the theater, rather than highlighting their potential contribution."* If you're using AI to speed up the project model — aggregating requests, generating roadmaps and PRDs and user stories — *"realize that an agent — or your engineer or designer — could just as easily do this themselves."*
- Cagan flags a footnote for an upcoming article on this as *"a serious anti-pattern"* — became the [[2026-06-04-cagan-pm-theater-age-of-ai-modern-cto|June 2026 Modern-CTO talk]].

### The limitations of human product coaching (§ diagnosis)
- **The coaching claim reasserted.** *"That was how I learned product, and it's how most of the best product people I know learned product."* Coaching is a top leadership principle at the consistently innovative product companies.
- **The transformation-trust link.** *"Until and unless the product leaders and the product creators can earn the trust of the stakeholders, little will actually change."* Normally it's effective coaching that gets product people to that respect. (Reinforces [[transformation-politics]].)
- **The bottleneck (verbatim).** *"So few product people have a manager that is both willing and able to provide this coaching."* Two reasons: never worked this way, or no time (span of control rising).
- **The load-bearing diagnosis:** *"We consider the lack of effective product coaching to be the primary obstacle to more people and organizations becoming strong at product, at the very time that so many companies are in serious need of these skills."*
- Training helps, external coaches help — *"but none of that is really a substitute for having someone that is both an expert in product craft, and also knows your company's strategic context, to be there to guide you for at least your first year or two."*

### The model as product coach (§ prescription)
- **SVPG's experimental arc.** Started with **custom GPTs**; moved to the **foundation models themselves** as they consistently improved.
- **Prompt engineering → context engineering.** Cagan's own verbatim reframe: *"As prompt engineering has evolved into context engineering, we have been learning just what context is necessary for the type of collaboration and engagement needed for effective product coaching."* Points at [[2020-01-09-cagan-coaching-strategic-context|Coaching – Strategic Context]] as the strategic-context reference.
- **The recommendation (verbatim):** *"We are now advocating that product creators and product leaders use the foundation models as their personal product coach."*
- **The manager-still-preferred caveat.** *"If your manager is willing and able to serve as a strong product coach, by all means you should take advantage of this, and consider yourself very fortunate."*
- **"At least as good as most managers."** For product creators, the answer to *"can it help most develop product sense and contribute at the level needed?"* is now **yes.** For product leaders — especially at scale — the setup is **model-as-coach *plus* a strong human product-leadership coach.**
- **The three named foundation models.** *"At least with the three major foundation models (Claude, Gemini and GPT)."* Frequency and severity of bad answers have dropped to *"reasonable to quite good."*
- **The prompt-key that ships in the article.** *"You will need to instruct your model to prioritize the operating model of product you are trying to learn (at a minimum, are you trying to learn the product model or the project model?)"* Elaborated as three keys in the April video.
- **Non-determinism warning.** *"A foundation model is not a deterministic product. The coaching advice you get today can be different tomorrow, and not necessarily better."* Also true, in fairness, of human coaches. SVPG is *"betting that the model-as-coach experience will continue to improve."*
- **The stance:** *"You are not there to blindly accept the statements and advice from the model; you need to question and seek real understanding, look for critique and mistakes rather than affirmation."*
- **Start-here recommendation:** [product sense](https://www.svpg.com/product-sense-demystified/). *"We are encouraging everyone to at least try this out."*

### 7×24 personal product coach (§ implication)
- **Global access line (verbatim):** *"Any aspiring product creator with the necessary [agency](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9DAPrL5mdY), whether that person is in San Francisco, or Sao Paulo, or Lagos, or anywhere else in the world with an internet connection and a connected device, now has 7×24 access to the advice and assistance of an experienced product coach, representing the aggregated learnings of some of the best minds in product."* The agency link points to Product Therapy Ep 1.
- **The full product-sense curriculum (verbatim).** *"After configuring your product coach, now you can start using the model-as-coach to learn about your company, your industry, your competitive landscape, your domain, the sales and marketing considerations, the financial considerations (both costs and monetization), the compliance, legal and privacy constraints, the key metrics used to assess your company's health, your different types of users and customers, your enabling technology, how your product team contributes to your overall product strategy, and how your team relates to other product teams."* This is a canonical curriculum for [[product-sense]] — quote it when coaching a PM through what to ask the model in their first weeks.

### Barriers to adoption (§ adoption curve)
- Same [[technology-adoption-life-cycle|technology adoption curve]] as the internet / PC / mobile — some companies moving as fast as they can, others hesitant on security and privacy grounds.
- Cagan's assessment: *"the impact of this technology is so dramatic compared to anything we've experienced, and the competitive disadvantage to abstaining is so painful, I am already seeing even conservative companies move forward more quickly than in the past."*

### Human product coaches (§ where humans concentrate)
- **SVPG's 5-year coach-network build.** *"Larger and better than ever. However, realistically, they represent a drop in the bucket compared to what the industry needs."*
- **Where SVPG concentrates human coaches now:** product leaders navigating [transformation politics](https://www.svpg.com/transformation-politics/) and building the four elements of [strategic context](https://www.svpg.com/coaching-strategic-context/) — product vision, product strategy, team topology, team objectives.
- **Product leadership is hard to do well.** For those who've never seen strong product leadership, this is [very hard to do well](https://www.svpg.com/product-leadership-is-hard/). The problems are mostly people problems — relationships and power dynamics — with strong product craft on top.
- **The "#1 fan" tension preserved (verbatim):** *"I am still the number one fan of human product coaching, but I think those of us that serve as human product coaches need to focus on where we can have the greatest impact, which is with the product leaders, and embrace the model-as-product-coach for the millions of product creators out there."*

### The zero-to-one problem for new product creators (§ a specific Cagan retraction)
- In his earlier [A Vision For Product Teams](https://www.svpg.com/a-vision-for-product-teams/), Cagan worried that experienced product creators would thrive but newcomers would find *"entry was blocked because the bar was too high for anyone that didn't already possess the necessary experience."*
- **The correction (verbatim):** *"Today I'm happy to say that I think I was wrong about that. I didn't envision that the models would be able to get good enough, fast enough, that they could help to dramatically accelerate the learning curve for aspiring product creators and product leaders."*
- Impact spans PMs, product designers, **and especially engineers.** With continuous coaching, *"you can progress dramatically faster than when you depended on your weekly 1:1 for your coaching."*

### The footnotes (not to skip)
1. The AI-as-project-model-accelerant is *"a serious anti-pattern"* — full treatment forthcoming (became the June 2026 Modern CTO talk).
2. **Why SVPG did NOT train a custom model.** Considered it; declined because (a) *"doing a good job with this would be a significant undertaking, and with the foundation models rapidly improving, the lifespan of that effort would likely be limited"*; and (b) philosophy: *"we view the model-as-coach more like our content — we want the knowledge freely available to everyone."*
3. **Anthropic Skills flagged as a possible next step** — *"the recent announcement of Skills from Anthropic will, we are hoping, represent yet another step forward."* Worth revisiting as Skills matures — potentially relevant to how this wiki is packaged and distributed later.
4. **The reference example.** *"You can find an example set of project files and project instructions at www.svpg.com/examples."*
