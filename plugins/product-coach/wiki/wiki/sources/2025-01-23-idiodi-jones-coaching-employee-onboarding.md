# Product Therapy Ep 13 — Coaching Employee Onboarding

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] (interviewed) and [[chris-jones|Chris Jones]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 13 · SVPG, January 23, 2025 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zMZbShibNAY). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. See also the SVPG article: [New Employee Bootcamp](https://www.svpg.com/new-employee-bootcamp/).

## Role
**Primary — the fullest first-person account of Idiodi's boot camp on record.** Jones interviews Idiodi (roles reversed for this episode). The **two-week structure**: **Week 1 = individual** (build trust between the leader and the new hire, share strategic context personally, tour the company, deliver a *user manual* for how the manager operates, and assign a **stakeholder buddy** — an emotional-intelligence black-belt move where an influential stakeholder invests two days teaching the new hire, borrowing that stakeholder's credibility while making them accountable for the new hire's competence). **Week 2 = team** (bring the actual team into the boot camp for a discovery-sprint-style safe practice — Sales / Marketing come in and present; the team practices collaboration before "game time"). **Milestones**: end of day 1 (frame the story the new hire tells their spouse); end of week 1 (frame the story they tell themselves over the weekend); **first paycheck** (deliver the *coaching plan* the day before — anchoring "I'm invested in getting you better while paying you like you're already great"); 30 / 90-day cycles. The *public win* discipline (a lesson Jones learned from his own manager): the hiring manager's job is to make sure the new hire gets a **public win** in the first 45 days.

## Notes for the coach
Two coaching principles worth surfacing: (a) **you are not hiring a finished product; you are hiring the raw ingredients** — the leader's day job is to build the product; hiring is only step one. (b) **you can re-onboard existing staff** — if your team never had a deliberate onboarding, you can design one today for people who have been there for years. Idiodi runs boot camps as **cohorts** (typically every 6–8 weeks in a hiring cycle) with hiring managers going through them alongside their new hires — so managers learn to coach onboarding while the new hires are onboarded.
