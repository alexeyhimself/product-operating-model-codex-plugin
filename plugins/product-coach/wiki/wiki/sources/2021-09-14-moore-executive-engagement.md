# Executive Engagement

> **Citation:** [[jon-moore|Jon Moore]] · *Executive Engagement* · SVPG, Sep 14, 2021 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/executive-engagement/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — Moore's post-*EMPOWERED* answer to *"how do senior leaders actually run the business while empowering teams?"*** Written in response to a growing number of senior leaders who read EMPOWERED, realized their command-and-control was the problem, and asked what to do differently.

Key material — Moore's **interaction principles** for senior-leader ↔ product-leader/team engagement:
- **Lead With Context Not Control** — push decisions down to who's closest to the enabling tech + customer; provide the strategic context (vision, strategy, financials, regulatory, industry trends, partnerships).
- **Outcomes not Output** — hold people to outcomes, only works when they're empowered to find the solution.
- **Knowing What You Can't Know** — with tech products, many things can't be known in advance; senior leadership admitting this is essential.
- **Data Beats Opinions** — the great equalizer; teach product teams to run tests to collect evidence; resolves differences among teams and with leadership.
- **High-Integrity Commitments** — the exception, not the rule; time and effort to make one is non-trivial; leaders need to understand this.
- **Nomenclature discipline** — "Senior Leaders" = CEO/Founders/CFO/COO/CMO/CRO/President; "Product Leaders" = managers/leaders of PM/design/eng. Distinct roles with distinct needs.
