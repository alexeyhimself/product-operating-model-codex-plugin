# Keys To Successful Transformation

> **Citation:** [[lea-hickman|Lea Hickman]] · *Keys To Successful Transformation* · SVPG, Jul 30, 2020 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/keys-to-successful-transformation/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the 2020 article-form root for the ten keys later formalized in [[transformed|TRANSFORMED]] Ch 48 (2024).** This is the article Hickman published to name what SVPG had come to believe is essential for a company to effectively transform — four years before the book.

Key material — the ten keys (as articulated in this 2020 article; later refined and re-numbered in TRANSFORMED Ch 48):
1. **Senior Leadership Support** — CEO-level active support; not just delegating "digital transformation" to a lieutenant.
2. **The Role of Technology** — from cost center to core enabler; impacts how tech is funded, staffed, and whether it's a core competency or outsourceable.
3. **Understand Impact Beyond Product** — transformation touches finance, HR, sales, marketing, customer success, business ops.
4. **Strong Product Leaders** — real product leaders, not project managers with a new title.
5. Additional keys follow (5–10 include Strong PMs, Designers, Empowered Engineers, Insights-Based Strategy, Stakeholder Collaboration, Continuous Evangelization of Outcomes, Corporate Courage).
