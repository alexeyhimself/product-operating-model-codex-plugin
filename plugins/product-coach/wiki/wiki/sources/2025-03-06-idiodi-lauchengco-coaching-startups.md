# Product Therapy Ep 16 — Coaching Startups

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 16 · SVPG, March 6, 2025 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrjxgCrfVdw). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the wiki's canonical teaching on product work inside early-stage startups.** Lauchengco brings her Costanoa Ventures operating-partner lens (10+ years, dozen+ boards, B2B and B2C). Key material: (a) **Ideas are dime-a-dozen; the readiness test is grit for market-side pressure testing.** *"If you're not prepared to talk to 100 people to pressure-test your idea, do not take your idea to VCs — that's the first thing you're going to be asked."* (b) **A great idea ≠ a great business** — VCs don't fund the idea, they fund *"the kernel of an exceptional business."* (c) **Market-product fit, not product-market fit** — Lauchengco and Jim Wilson's multi-part series argues the *market* side is what most product-background founders under-invest in. Coaching pattern: if the founder is product-background, coach them on market; if marketing-background, coach them on product. (d) **The 9–10x adoption bar (Andreessen/Horowitz)** — you are displacing an existing product or behavior, and the switch drags every adjacent stakeholder along with it. Corollary: "they only use Excel" is a *warning*, not a permission — Excel is the most-customized, most-mastered tool on the planet. Whiz example: *"turn this one thing on, turn these six other things off"* — the fastest-approved security purchase in history. (e) **Great-founder traits** — growth mindset, humility, and (Lauchengco's crisp indicator) *"do they run into breaches when they see a gap, or do they stay on their side and complain about the gap?"* (f) **Co-founders — 100% of the time.** Every solo founder Lauchengco has worked with regrets it in retrospect. Look for intellectual-equal + complementary skills, not clone. (g) **The single startup focus is [[market-fit|product-market fit]].** Kingdom-building (marketing dept, sales dept, org chart) before PMF is an absolute distraction. (h) **First hire is contextual + opportunistic** — Davey-Crockett-first-sales-rep type. (i) **Joining a startup — the four assessments**: the team; quality of revenue (churn, retention, logos, pipeline — not top-line); backer quality as a proxy for due-diligence; your own passion for the space. (j) **Startup vs enterprise culture** — enterprises have unavoidable political undercurrents; startups don't yet, which is a *feature* for some and stress for others. (k) **Leading indicators (pre-PMF)** — how fast the team's problem list turns over. Winners move through problems fast; losers cling to a problem or a belief-about-how-it-should-be. **Post-PMF** — adoption speed + customer quality. (l) **Idiodi's crisp reframe:** *"the number one reason you run out of money is you're not trying many things fast enough — 6-to-9-month MVPs against the ~50-attempt-to-PMF benchmark = you'll run out of money."* (m) **Lauchengco's investment stance:** *"we invest in the people, not the idea — the idea/product/market/space all change; the people have to have shown they can operate that environment."*
