# Breaking Down Walls

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] · *Breaking Down Walls* · SVPG, Jun 2, 2023 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/breaking-down-walls/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. Includes editorial note from [[marty-cagan|Cagan]] framing this as both an argument about transformation and a tribute to Jeremiah Ivan.

## Role
**Primary — Idiodi's tribute to Jeremiah Ivan (VP Engineering at Merrill Corp / Datasite)** and a case study in the head-of-product / head-of-technology partnership as the load-bearing relationship in a transformation. Cagan's editorial note: *"I considered him one of the best VPE/CTO's I've ever had the opportunity to work with."*

Key material:
- **The setup.** Idiodi as newly appointed Head of Product at Merrill Corporation (legacy financial printer). Daunting transformation mandate. VP Engineering: Jeremiah Ivan.
- **The physical metaphor.** Ten-foot-high cubicles inhibiting collaboration. Idiodi and Ivan agree they have to go — "Jeremiah Ivan was the king of breaking down walls — sometimes with brute force."
- **Shared vision.** The head-of-product ↔ head-of-engineering partnership as the pattern; shared vision + team troubleshooting.
- **Tribute context.** Ivan passed away; Idiodi worked with him at two different companies. Article is simultaneously a transformation piece and a personal memorial.
