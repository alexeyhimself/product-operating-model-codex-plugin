# Product Therapy Ep 15 — Coaching Remote Collaboration

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[lea-hickman|Lea Hickman]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 15 · SVPG, February 20, 2025 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iCzzlMPEEbM). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the wiki's canonical teaching on remote product collaboration.** SVPG's historical stance (collocation as default) restated by Hickman, then extended into the reality that most product orgs are remote or hybrid now. Key material the podcast contributes: (a) **the rules of proximity** — you optimize for the biggest gap (customer-discovery gap → proximity to customers; go-to-market alignment gap → proximity to GTM team); Hickman's Bangalore/SF example (moved PM and design to Bangalore for engineering proximity, budgeted quarterly US trips for customer proximity). (b) **The "always-on" technique** (Idiodi's method): camera on at start of workday, self-view *off*, act as though the team is collocated — ask questions across the room, go to the bathroom naturally, say goodbye at end of day. Corollary: **Zoom fatigue is caused by seeing yourself, not by seeing others.** (c) **The false-trust trap:** in remote meetings, small talk substitutes for real rapport — the team tries to build "enough trust for the 1-hour timebox"; higher-performing teams that actually work together don't need it. (d) **Anti-pattern: collaborating through an artifact** — the async 6-pager comment-storm where alignment lives in the comments. Hickman's rule: *"documentation is about capturing a conversation, not being the vehicle for alignment."* Petrifies PMs from writing narratives at all. (e) **True collaboration = solving a problem together** — vs meetings (status / info-share / updates); Idiodi's rule: *"work is when you create work product — insights, code, a decision, an outcome."* (f) **The best collaboration tool is the phone.** Idiodi's example: email → call-back → 19–90 seconds → decision → move on. Scheduling a meeting to make a decision has lost the game before it starts. (g) **Debate is natural to product; collaboration ≠ consensus.** Hickman's "disagree and commit" culture with engineering-led company that transformed. (h) **The sports-huddle metaphor** (Idiodi): everybody in the huddle whether the play is called or not; shared context is rule #1; nothing says a goalkeeper can't score. *"There's nothing I should know that my designer or my engineer should not know."* (i) **Blame games are structural** — the fix is a shared *quantifiable* definition of success + shared access to the same data (customers, product telemetry, financial). Worst anti-pattern: engineering measured on velocity while product is measured on results. (j) **Leader-level collaboration** — tie every VP's objectives to the transformation's success (Hickman's HR-driven fix), otherwise VPs optimize for their own silo objectives and there is *no incentive* to help each other.

## Notes for the coach
Attribute the *always-on with self-view off* technique to Idiodi (Ep 15 is the canonical podcast statement). Attribute the *rules of proximity* framework and the *"documentation is about capturing a conversation, not the vehicle for alignment"* rule to Hickman. When a team reports "we're just not clicking as a remote team," probe first for the false-trust trap (small-talk → work → repeat, without ever actually collaborating), then for the artifact-collaboration anti-pattern (async comment threads standing in for real discussion). The Hickman VP-objectives-tied-to-transformation fix is a leader-level move — cite [[keys-to-successful-transformation]] and the HR mechanism. Preserve the podcast disclaimer (findahelpline.com).
