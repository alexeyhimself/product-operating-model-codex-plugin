# Idiodi — On Product Sense: Integrating Data and Intuition (Product Momentum)

> **Citation:** Christian Idiodi (SVPG) interviewed by Paul Gebel and Dan Sharp, *Product Momentum* ep. 142, *On Product Sense: Integrating Data and Intuition* · YouTube, published 2024-06-25 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yLKEmYOPxkI). Idiodi's second appearance on the show. Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## What it backs
- concepts: [[product-sense]] (**Idiodi's definition** — not a gift; the cultivated by-product of deep knowledge of customer, business, industry, data and product, which is why organisations trust that person's judgment. His sports analogy: "when in doubt they give it to their superstar" — the star is trusted because of practised reps, not innate talent. Pairs with, and is more operational than, the [[2024-05-02-idiodi-cagan-coaching-product-sense|*Product Therapy* Ep 3]] treatment), [[emotional-intelligence]] ("human skills", not "soft skills" — self-awareness, EQ, relationship-building and influence, time management, leadership), [[ai-and-product-teams]] (the argument that if product sense were only data and inputs, an LLM would have the best product sense; what is *right for a human, now, in this context* stays a human judgment), [[transformation]] (the CFO framing — a two-week sprint of a product team costs real money; the best companies trust that spend to the person who knows most, which is what a PM is *for*)
- competencies: [[product-manager]], [[product-leadership]]
- entities: [[christian-idiodi]]

## Notes for the coach
- **The "companies drift from helping people to extracting value from them" framing** is Idiodi's own and is the moral spine of this episode: product management is the discipline that *holds the contract of value creation* — make customers happy **while** making the business money; doing either alone is the lazy option. Complements [[2024-08-27-cagan-the-moral-case-for-the-product-model]].
- **Why the books exist, in his words:** *INSPIRED* for product teams, *EMPOWERED* for leaders creating the environment, *TRANSFORMED* for the C-suite / CEO, because each layer turned out to be a ceiling on the one below. The most compact statement of the series' logic in the video corpus; backs [[recommended-library]] and the four book entity pages.
- **Collaborative problem-solving over problem-solving.** Idiodi is explicit that he does not want lone problem-solvers: the handy relative who can fix anything alone is *not* the profile. Knowing when to ask for help is part of competence. Backs [[the-product-team-trio]] and [[collaboration]].
- Recorded ~3 months after *TRANSFORMED* shipped; the closing segment is a book pitch and can be skipped.
