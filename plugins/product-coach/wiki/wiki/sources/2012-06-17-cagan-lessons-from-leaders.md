# Cagan — Lessons From Leaders

> **Citation:** Marty Cagan · "Lessons From Leaders" · svpg.com, 2012-06-17 — [read the original](https://www.svpg.com/lessons-from-leaders/). Slim provenance card; the article is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** Cagan's list of 10 favorite company mantras collected during company visits — quotes that *"resonate strongly with people and help them internalize important concepts or ideas."* The article names attributions where Cagan can, though he flags the tracing problem: *"tracking down the original author of these types of quotes is hard."* Useful when the wiki wants to cite a specific mantra to a specific company culture rather than to a book chapter.

## What it backs
- concepts: [[product-culture]] — the mechanism (mantras as culture-carriers); Cagan uses these mantras to *"understand their culture and their values."*
- concepts: [[product-vision]] — mantra 2 ("Making a profit is never the purpose, it is simply an outcome" — Simon Sinek) and mantra 3 ("Think in leaps, but iterate in steps" — Google).
- principles: [[test-ideas-responsibly]] — mantra 6 (*"If you're not testing, you're guessing"*), and mantra 7 (*"Fake it before you make it"* — Google, on prototype-first work). Also grounds [[prototypes]].
- concepts: [[missionaries-vs-mercenaries]] — mantra 10 (*"The company cares about what the leader cares about"* — [[bill-campbell|Bill Campbell]]). Cagan applies this recursively: *"the team cares about what the product manager cares about."*
- entities: [[amazon]] — mantras 1 and 4: *"Our job is to invent on behalf of our users"* and *"Be stubborn on vision but flexible on details"*. The latter is the Bezos-line the wiki quotes elsewhere.
- entities: [[google]] — mantras 3, 7, 9 (*"Data beats opinions"*). Article's parallel quote from Jim Barksdale at Netscape: *"If you don't have any facts, we'll just use my opinion."*
- entities: [[bill-campbell]] — mantra 10.

## Notes for the coach
- **The 10 mantras, exactly** (Cagan's attributions kept):
  1. **"Our job is to invent on behalf of our users."** — Amazon. Grounds the wiki's *customers don't know what's possible* claim.
  2. **"Making a profit is never the purpose, it is simply an outcome."** — Simon Sinek. Cagan applies it to both vision-need and product/P&L relationship.
  3. **"Think in leaps, but iterate in steps."** — Google. Reconciles vision + Agile.
  4. **"Be stubborn on vision but flexible on details."** — Amazon (Bezos). Where to persist vs where to iterate. On [[product-vision]].
  5. **"Don't fall in love."** — Facebook. The attachment-to-ideas anti-pattern. Complement to mantra 4.
  6. **"If you're not testing, you're guessing."** — no single attribution; Cagan sees it everywhere.
  7. **"Fake it before you make it."** — Google. Prototypes-first.
  8. **"If you continue to improve a product enough, you'll eventually ruin it."** — David Pogue (NYT). The feature-chasing trap.
  9. **"Data beats opinions."** — Google. Companion Barksdale line: *"If you don't have any facts, we'll just use my opinion."*
  10. **"The company cares about what the leader cares about."** — [[bill-campbell|Bill Campbell]]. **Recursive application: "the team cares about what the product manager cares about"** — Cagan's own extension in the article. Quote it directly on [[product-manager]] and [[product-culture]] when talking about leader-attention as a cultural signal.
- **Attribution honesty.** Cagan admits he cannot verify origins for most of these — *"I can't be confident of the origination of any of these"* — so treat attributions as *company-associated, not company-authored*.
- **Age of the piece.** 2012 — one of the earliest SVPG articles the wiki carries. Testing/prototyping mantras (6, 7) predate the *INSPIRED 2nd ed* fuller build-out of [[product-discovery]]; the [[prototypes]] page has more recent primary sources for the operational form.
