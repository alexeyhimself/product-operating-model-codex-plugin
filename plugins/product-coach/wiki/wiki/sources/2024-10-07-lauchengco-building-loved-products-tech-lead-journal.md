> **Citation:** [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] with [Henry Suryawirawan] · *Tech Lead Journal — Building Loved Products: The Tech Product Marketing Fundamentals* · Oct 7, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jC48ZCEZBA). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — Lauchengco's fullest tech-lead-audience spoken articulation of the LOVED fundamentals**, aimed at engineers and tech leads (the audience who typically thinks PMM is not their concern). The opening quote is Lauchengco's crispest reformulation: *"Product marketing's purpose is to drive product adoption by shaping market perception through strategic marketing activities that align with business goals."*

Key material (from show notes):
- **Product marketing's purpose.** Driving product adoption by shaping market perception through strategic marketing activities aligned with business goals — the "shaping market perception" framing is Lauchengco's own.
- **The mistake most tech teams make.** Focusing too much on features / not enough on perception.
- **The four fundamentals (LOVED framing).** [[pmm-ambassador|Ambassador]] · [[pmm-strategist|Strategist]] · [[pmm-storyteller|Storyteller]] · [[pmm-evangelist|Evangelist]] — the *LOVED* Ch 3–6 framework in spoken form.
- **The tech-lead audience frame.** Why engineers should care about PMM (product adoption depends on perception; perception depends on GTM; GTM depends on PMM collaboration).
