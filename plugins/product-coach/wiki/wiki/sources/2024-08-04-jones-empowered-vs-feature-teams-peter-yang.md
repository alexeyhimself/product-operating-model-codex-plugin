# Jones — Real Talk about Empowered vs. Feature Product Teams (Peter Yang)

> **Citation:** Chris Jones (SVPG) interviewed by Peter Yang, *Real Talk about Empowered vs. Feature Product Teams* · YouTube, published 2024-08-04 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h0wJbvfnmlg). Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** [[chris-jones|Jones]] answering the *"empowered is a feel-good word"* backlash head-on. The single best spoken statement of the wiki's **empowerment is not a free-for-all** position: leadership allocates the problems; the team owns the solution. Root source stays [[transformed]] and [[empowered]]; this is the clarifying gloss.

## Notes for the coach
- **The two symmetric leadership failures**, both named in one passage: the leader who reverts to command-and-control under stress ("hands on the wheel"), and the leader who steps so far back they don't know what the team is doing. *"What we need is the leader that really knows when to lean in and how to lean in and when to step back."*
- **The three-category cut of the model** (how you build · how you solve problems · how you decide which problems to solve) is stated here as *product operating principles*, before the "three dimensions" phrasing settled. Same content as [[2022-05-31-moore-transformation-defined|Moore's canonical article]].
- **Bottom-up move for a disempowered PM:** you may not get to stop receiving solutions, but you can always ask *"what does success look like?"* and get an outcome attached to the feature. Jones calls this the first rung of empowerment. Backs [[coaching-up]].
- **On failure:** a launch that misses is only a true failure if there's **no insight** in it. "We took our best shot and it was a crapshoot" is the bad answer, because nothing can be done with it. Backs [[learning-over-failure]].
- Contains Jones's commentary on **Airbnb / Chesky** (removing the PM title doesn't remove value and viability — someone still owns them) and **Google** — flagged by Jones himself as outside-in speculation, *not* firsthand. Do not cite the Google passage as an SVPG assessment; the wiki's Google material belongs to [[google-product-model]].
