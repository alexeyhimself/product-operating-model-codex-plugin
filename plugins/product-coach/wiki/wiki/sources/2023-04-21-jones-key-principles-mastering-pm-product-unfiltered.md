# Key Principles for Mastering Product Management — Product Unfiltered

> **Citation:** [[chris-jones|Chris Jones]] on *Product Unfiltered* · Apr 21, 2023 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H3UafspuzpA). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. Companion writeup: [pmunfiltered.com/post/mastering-product-management-chris-jones-partner-svpg](https://www.pmunfiltered.com/post/mastering-product-management-chris-jones-partner-svpg).

## Role
**Primary — Jones's fullest spoken PM-mastery narrative from 2023** — the evolution of PM since the early 90s; the data + user-experience emphasis; empowering teams to solve problems (not build features); why individual PMs are limited in old-operating-model companies; Adobe as the multi-decade transformation exemplar; the iPhone / Yahoo / Netscape historical narrative; generative AI compared to the calculator's arrival.

Key material (from show notes):
- **The evolution of product management since the early 90s** — how PMs then vs now differ (Jones's own career started at Adobe circa Photoshop 1.0).
- **Data + user experience** as the dominant emphasis.
- **Empowering teams to solve problems, not build features** — the empowerment thesis restated.
- **The limitation of individual PMs in old-operating-model companies** — a PM can't singlehandedly overcome a broken operating model.
- **Adobe as multi-decade transformation exemplar** — Adobe's ability to transform and lead its market for decades; also Adobe's recent attempt to enter the podcast industry with a user-friendly, no-training product.
- **iPhone impact + touch screen rise.**
- **Yahoo's early internet domination and inability to adapt** — the curated-model failure to the explosive-growth internet.
- **Generative AI compared to the calculator's arrival** — freeing us from certain problems, forcing new skills.
