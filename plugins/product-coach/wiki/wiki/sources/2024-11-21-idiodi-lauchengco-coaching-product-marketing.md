# Product Therapy Ep 10 — Coaching Product Marketing

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[martina-lauchengco|Martina Lauchengco]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 10 · SVPG, November 21, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UFYlvHKnAqI). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** Lauchengco's compact restatement of *LOVED*'s core thesis for a podcast audience: **product marketing = driving product adoption by shaping market perception through strategic marketing activities that connect to business goals.** *"It's the steel thread that unites the product with the go-to-market machinery."* The wiki's canonical **Microsoft AI market-shaping story**: from 2021 Google-was-the-AI-leader → 2023 Microsoft-shaped-the-narrative through the ChatGPT/Bing beta with unrestricted influencer access, Satya Nadella's *"make Google dance"* gauntlet, and the AI-across-Enterprise reframe. Not a search-engine play; a positioning play whose business goal was Enterprise-AI trust. **Healthy PM ↔ PMM collaboration** = *"both feel they are better at their job because of the other"* — the PMM brings market signal into prioritization; the PM brings product juice into GTM. **PMM without a PMM**: the work must be done by the founders, the sales team, or the PM — the discipline is what matters, not the title.

## Notes for the coach
Two Lauchengco reframes worth naming explicitly: (a) **product value gets defined through a use case** — early-stage adoption depends on a hyper-specific use case someone can name back to you; (b) *"you don't get to change the market — the market is what it is; you bridge into it, provide context, and frame value."* On checklists-vs-strategy: *"product marketers who check every box are not doing the job — the job is to be selective about what's most important to say"* — a warning against the *"average product marketing"* pattern of doing-it-all-but-moving-nothing. Attribute the Microsoft narrative to Lauchengco; the *"who does the CEO turn to for growth?"* rhetorical framing runs through Idiodi's broader coaching.
