## Notes for the coach
- **Idiodi's framing is the citable claim for the wiki:** IQ is entry-level for product work; EQ is the higher-order competency, and it is *foundational to the craft*, not adjacent to it — better EQ makes you better at customer interviews, at testing, at running meetings, at OKR sessions, at roadmaps. He offers this and Leto does not debug it.
- **Weak-EQ tells** worth using diagnostically: blaming others / low accountability · needing to be right · not accepting criticism · "flipping your lid" (Dan Siegel's hand model of the brain, cited) · shutting down. Leto's point is that the outburst matters less than the response to it, and that emotions are contagious enough to shape a culture.
- **This is coachable and it is slow.** Leto is direct that EQ, unlike IQ, is developable, and equally direct that it takes commitment and time and is very hard to do alone.
- **Disclaimer carried by the show itself:** *Product Therapy* is not hosted by mental-health professionals and is not a substitute for professional support. Preserve that framing if any of this material is used in a coaching context.
