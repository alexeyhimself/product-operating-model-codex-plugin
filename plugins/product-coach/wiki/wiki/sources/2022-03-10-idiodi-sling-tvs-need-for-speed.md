# Sling TV's Need for Speed

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] · *Sling TV's Need for Speed* · SVPG, Mar 10, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/sling-tvs-need-for-speed-strong/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local. Paired podcast: *Rocketship — Digital Transformations: Sling TV and Ordnance Survey*.

## Role
**Primary — Idiodi's Sling TV case featuring Paddy Rao (SVP Strategy & Technology)** — a legacy pay-TV incumbent that continues to transform as its DNA. Complements [[2013-07-21-cagan-the-need-for-speed|Cagan's *The Need for Speed*]] canonical article with a named-company worked example.

Key material:
- **Sling TV.** Streaming TV service, wholly-owned subsidiary of Dish Network. Launched as the first OTT service provider in mid-2015.
- **Paddy Rao (SVP Strategy & Technology) quote.** *"Sling has a tradition of reinventing itself. We invented the first pay-television market. Then we invented Sling TV, and now we're inventing the 5G entertainment network. The rule of thumb for us is that if you stop transforming, you stop growing."*
- **The 50–100x speed multiplier.** *"On average, we are now about 50 to 100 times faster than we were two years ago."*
- **Speed as outcome, culture as driver.** Sling's CPAW acronym — **C**uriosity · **P**ride · **A**dventure · **W**inning — alignment with these values is what Sling hires for.
- **SRE as consequence.** For most people, TV is not entertainment — it's essential service. Sling has to be up and running 24×7.

When citing Sling TV, attribute the 50–100x speed claim and the CPAW acronym to Paddy Rao directly (Idiodi quotes him). Note: Sling is a rare *incumbent* speed exemplar — usually speed narratives come from startups/challengers. Use when a client claims speed is only for greenfield companies.
