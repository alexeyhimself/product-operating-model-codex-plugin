# Cagan — Common Transformation Pitfalls (ProductTank Oslo)

> **Citation:** Marty Cagan, *Common Transformation Pitfalls (and Q&A)*, ProductTank Oslo (Mind the Product) · YouTube, published 2022-04-13 — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X94m-DAgmlE). Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon).** Cagan's fullest spoken taxonomy of *why transformations fail*, given ~two years before *TRANSFORMED* shipped. Two explicit top-ten lists — **external to product & technology** (the harder ones) and **internal** (the easier ones) — plus the Guardian *eyewitness* story as his worked answer to "what does a successful transformation look like?" Attribute the model itself to [[transformed]]; this talk is the pre-book articulation of the failure modes and is unusually blunt about them.

## Notes for the coach
- **The external list is the point.** Cagan is explicit that these are mostly outside a product person's control and that *"most of this talk is for CEOs"*: command-and-control addiction · a designated Chief Digital Officer as the CEO's way of not dealing with it · hiring McKinsey/Bain/BCG for board cover · sales-driven product · marketing-driven product · CFO project-based funding · predictability addiction · **reporting to a CIO (cost centre) rather than a CTO (profit centre)** · outsourced engineering · overwhelming tech debt.
- **The internal list:** the desire for process (Europe called out by name) · inadequate product managers / the product-owner title · engineers who "just want to code" · tools that smuggle in a process · too much keep-the-lights-on work · too many initiatives · too much tech debt · too much pressure · more work · outcomes are hard.
- **Useful counterweight:** empowered teams are *more* pressure and *more* stress than feature teams, by design — and some people rationally opt out. Cagan says this plainly here in a way the books soften.
- **Cagan's own coaching origin story** is told at length at the end (HP; the coach who barred him from decisions until he'd visited 30 customers; the go-to-market ride-alongs; the finance tutor; LTV). This is the most detailed first-person version of it in the video corpus and backs [[coaching-the-pm]] and [[product-coaching]].
- Dated 2022, so it **predates** the "three dimensions" vocabulary being standard; the failure taxonomy here is organised by *who causes it*, not by dimension. Don't retrofit.
