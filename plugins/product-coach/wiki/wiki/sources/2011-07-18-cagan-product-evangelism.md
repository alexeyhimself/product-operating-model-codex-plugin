# Product Evangelism

> **Citation:** [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Product Evangelism* · SVPG, Jul 18, 2011 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/product-evangelism/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the SVPG article-form root for the [[product-evangelism]] framework page.** The wiki's [[product-evangelism]] page currently cites *INSPIRED* Ch 31 as the book-form source; this article is Cagan's 2011 SVPG article version of the same material, published years before *INSPIRED* 2nd ed's Ch 31 crystallized it.

Key material:
- Product evangelism as "selling the dream" (Guy Kawasaki attribution).
- The evangelism-not-optional argument for both startup founders and large-company PMs — without it, the product will "get killed before it sees the light of day."
- The **ten techniques** for "selling the dream": (1) high-fidelity prototype · (2) share the pain (bring devs/stakeholders to user testing) · (3) share the vision (2–3 year timeframe) · (4) share learnings generously · (5) share credit generously · (6) learn to give a great demo · (7) do your homework · (8) be genuinely excited · (9) show enthusiasm · (10) spend time with the team (face time with every designer/dev/QA).
- The PM-as-owner framing: *"I've always believed that the product owner needs to be the product evangelist for the team."*
