# Dedicated Product Teams

> **Citation:** [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Dedicated Product Teams* · SVPG, Feb 18, 2010 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/dedicated-product-teams/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — one of the earliest SVPG articles establishing the *dedicated* product team as the alternative to project-based feature teams.** Predates the 2019 [[2019-08-29-cagan-product-vs-feature-teams|*Product vs Feature Teams*]] article by nine years and the [[2018-10-31-cagan-empowered-product-teams|*Empowered Product Teams*]] article by eight; the same durability-vs-empowerment idea in its earliest wiki-attested form.

Key material:
- The two dominant ways of running a product org: (1) **project-based planning with feature teams** (management defines a communal roadmap; teams get allocated to projects for weeks/months; teams and domain change frequently); (2) **dedicated product teams** (a durable team owns a persistent problem/product area over time).
- Advocates dedicated teams as the fix for the "wall between product management and engineering" that Cagan wrote about in his prior article *Knocking Down Walls*.
- Explicitly frames dedicated teams as a technique with multiple benefits, not merely an org chart change.
