# Coaching – Imposter Syndrome

> **Citation:** [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Coaching – Imposter Syndrome* · SVPG, Oct 15, 2019 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/coaching-imposter-syndrome/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the article-form root of [[imposter-syndrome]]**, five years earlier than the *Product Therapy* Ep 6 podcast ([[2024-07-25-idiodi-jones-coaching-imposter-syndrome]]) the page had been rooted on. Cagan opens by calling his own view *"a bit of a contrarian opinion."*

Key material:
- **The contrarian reframe.** Imposter syndrome is real *and* "imposters are also a real thing." Cagan treats the feeling not as an irrational fear to push past but as **a signal to be obeyed**: *"It is my mind warning me of the consequences if I don't do my homework and truly prepare."*
- **Against "overcoming" it.** *"Should they be praised for overcoming their stage fright fears and getting in front of a group? To me that's like giving a child a trophy just for showing up to the game."*
- **The remedy is preparation plus honest review** — testing the talk or article beforehand on people you respect who *will* tell you the truth: *"more than a few times, those people have indeed saved me from myself."*
- **The frustration is aimed at the manager, not the PM.** On seeing an underwhelming executive presentation: *"my frustration is centered not on the product manager, but rather on that person's manager."* Did she review a draft or rehearsal? Provide honest feedback? Arrange progressive speaking opportunities?
- **Why it's a trust problem, not a confidence problem.** *"Empowered product teams are predicated on trust… When a product manager appears unprepared, or naïve, in front of the executives, that trust is diminished, and it will take a long time to regain."*
- **The leader's yardstick:** *"they are only as strong as their weakest product manager."*

## Notes for the coach
Useful precisely because it **inverts the usual advice**. Most imposter-syndrome coaching aims to reassure; Cagan's aim is to convert the anxiety into preparation and then into a feedback loop with trusted reviewers. Pair with [[2024-07-25-idiodi-jones-coaching-imposter-syndrome|Ep 6]], which covers the same ground in a warmer, vulnerability-first register — the two are complementary, not contradictory, but a coachee who is genuinely struggling will hear this article as harsh if given without the podcast's framing.

Note also the in-passing swipe at the state of the field (*"how many people… are, at least in my personal opinion, advocating nonsense"*), which links to the same concern that later becomes [[product-management-theater]].
