# Idiodi — Building Strong Product Cultures (Product Unfiltered)

> **Citation:** Christian Idiodi (SVPG) on *Product Unfiltered*, *Building Strong Product Cultures* · YouTube, published 2023-05-30 (pre-*TRANSFORMED*) — [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bS42yJNDTCA) · [episode page](https://www.pmunfiltered.com/post/building-strong-product-cultures-christian-idiodi). Slim provenance card; transcript **not reproduced**.

## What it backs
- concepts: [[product-culture]] (**the load-bearing distinction of this episode: product culture ≠ company culture.** Amazon is Idiodi's example of a strong *product* culture he would hesitate to recommend as a *company* culture. The asymmetry he states: a good product can come out of a bad **company** culture, but not out of a bad **product** culture — and a bad company culture will erode a good product culture over time, usually via process and promotion), [[process-people]], [[empowered-product-teams]], [[ai-and-product-teams]] (his 2023 read: AI absorbs elementary tasks in every discipline and will not replace the disciplines; the lawyer-who-uses-AI framing), [[technology-adoption-life-cycle]]
- frameworks: [[team-objectives]] / [[okrs]] (**the three things a leader owes a team with a problem**: *what* the problem is, *why* it matters, and *how success will be measured* — plus the **constraints/health metrics** you must not damage while pursuing it. His "buy every customer a boat" reductio is the memorable version of why customer delight alone is trivially easy and meaningless)
- competencies: [[product-manager]] (**the doctor analogy** — the core competency is diagnose-then-prescribe; specialisations exist, but every doctor can do CPR)
- case-studies / entities: [[spotify]] (as an example of solutions that serve the business while customers still love them), [[christian-idiodi]]

## Notes for the coach
- **Where the problems come from, precisely:** *"the problems teams work on should come from leadership, and those leaders should get the insight for what problems from their strategy."* Then the team decides how. Idiodi states this as neither top-down nor bottom-up but "a little of both" — the same anti-free-for-all correction [[chris-jones|Jones]] makes in [[2024-08-04-jones-empowered-vs-feature-teams-peter-yang]].
- **Nokia / Motorola / BlackBerry vs. the first iPhone** is his focus-group cautionary tale: they asked customers what they wanted in the next phone and were told *don't touch my keyboard*. Use it on [[product-discovery]] / [[discovery-techniques-catalog]] as the standard argument against focus groups in tech products — same argument Cagan makes; Idiodi's version is unusually concrete about the three companies.
- **B2B vs B2C, framed as constraints rather than as different jobs:** the work is identical (solve a problem customers love in a way that works for the business); what differs is the *viability constraint set* — B2B carries heavy sellability/compliance/regulatory constraints, B2C optimises for the smallest unit of value with the fewest constraints. Useful for [[product-marketing-by-stage]] and [[the-four-big-risks]].
- Recorded 2023, so his "15 years / 200 products" self-description and his AI commentary both predate the current SVPG AI canon — cite the AI passage as an early datapoint only, not as current position ([[2026-04-17-idiodi-cagan-coaching-in-the-age-of-ai]] and the Batch 1–2 AI cards carry that).
