# Product Therapy Ep 5 — Coaching Ethics

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[jon-moore|Jon Moore]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 5 · SVPG, June 27, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6YMYpt1IXPo). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary.** Frames ethics as an emergent **fifth risk** (alongside value, usability, feasibility, and viability), overlapping most with viability — but not reducible to it. Ownership: *"if everyone owns it, no one owns it"* — leaders set the culture; **the PM is the person at the front line** because they see the design/prototype before it ships. Moore's China-launch story (writing a jira ticket for a Communications service he was ambivalent about) grounds the *psychological safety and disagree-and-commit* pattern for ethical questions. **Good ethics is good business** — the incoming European regulation of social media (and probable smartphone age limits) is Moore's argument that companies get an ethics call wrong at their own commercial peril. **Diversity is upstream of ethics** — a homogeneous room can't spot what only a different perspective would flag.

## Notes for the coach
Idiodi's West-Africa fintech example (contact-list shaming for loan defaults) is the wiki's canonical case of *the easy solution being the wrong solution* — a hard-problem framing directly parallel to the [[the-four-big-risks|Four Big Risks]] work. On generative AI: Moore's stance is *"technology is a Force for good, and we live in a test-and-learn world — but we have to actually fix what we break, faster than we did with social media."* Attribute the fifth-risk framing to Moore; the *"trust IS the product hack"* rhetorical line runs through Idiodi's coaching more broadly.
