# Cagan — Transformed FAQ

> **Citation:** Marty Cagan · "Transformed FAQ" · svpg.com, 2023-11-28 — [read the original](https://www.svpg.com/transformed-faq/). Slim provenance card; the article is **not reproduced** here.

## Role
**Primary (SVPG canon) — the running FAQ companion to *[[transformed|TRANSFORMED]]*.** A rolling Q&A of objections and questions that came up after publication, framed as the sequel to the book's own "Objections" section. Mostly reinforces canon already ingested elsewhere in the wiki (product ops's six definitions; the CEO-support question as a progressive trust game; "is a bet just a feature at another level?"); the genuinely new nuggets are the **feature-team-is-sometimes-enough exceptions** and the explicit **individual-contributor-can-matter** answer.

## What it backs
- diagnostics: [[feature-team-vs-empowered-team]] (new — the two named exceptions where a feature team is genuinely sufficient: a bug-fix team, and a re-platforming/tech-debt team once the architecture decisions are settled)
- concepts: [[coaching-agency]] (supporting — an IC up-leveling her own skills from feature-team PM to empowered-team PM is itself a career-advancing act, not dependent on the CEO being on board first), [[team-topology]] (supporting — the product model is orthogonal to org structure; you usually don't need to reorganize to adopt it — the fuller treatment is *The Product Model and Org Design*, ingest-plan Batch 6), [[team-objectives]] (supporting — "a bet is an outcome you are seeking [a problem]; a feature is output [a particular solution]; a bet at one level can be a feature at another")
- concepts (reinforces, no new content needed): [[product-ops]] (the six Product Ops definitions, already rooted on [[2021-12-20-cagan-product-ops-overview]]), [[transformation-politics]] (the CEO-trust-as-multi-level-game framing, consistent with existing canon)

## Notes for the coach
- **The feature-team exceptions (new to the wiki).** *"Yes, there are some cases where a feature team is enough."* Two named examples: (1) a **bug-fix team** — there is rarely a need for product discovery on a bug fix; (2) a **tech-debt / re-platforming team**, at least once the major technology decisions have already been made and it's a matter of "plowing through the code base." Both are useful, don't need the extra capabilities of an empowered product team, and this is not a case for "up-leveling everything regardless of fit."
- **The IC-impact answer (verbatim, paraphrased).** No, an individual contributor cannot single-handedly transform a company — but Cagan has "pointed out many times that an individual contributor has more ability to impact her work environment than most people realize," and up-leveling from a feature-team PM to an empowered-team PM often leads to recognition and promotion; from there, a product leader has much more ability to impact the broader company.
- **The CEO-support question — a multi-level game, not a binary.** Trust is earned progressively: a PM proves herself with her own skills, then with her team, then a product leader across a set of teams, until a business unit or the full enterprise has transformed. Reinforces (does not add to) the existing [[transformation-politics|transformation-politics]] canon.
- **The "bet vs. feature at another level" framing.** A bet is an outcome you're seeking (a problem to be solved); a feature is output (a particular solution someone wants built). The metaphor for product strategy — invest teams in the areas the strategy names, and give as much latitude as possible in how they solve it.
- **The best-companies heuristic, restated (no new content).** Consistent innovation, demonstrated over time and recognized by the market (Amazon, Apple, Google, Netflix, Slack, Spotify, Stripe) — matches the wiki's existing exemplar list on [[overview]] and [[model-maturity]]; not re-cited there to avoid redundant sourcing.
- **Product Ops, six definitions — reinforces, not new.** Points to the same overview article ([[2021-12-20-cagan-product-ops-overview]]) already rooting [[product-ops]]; no page change needed.
