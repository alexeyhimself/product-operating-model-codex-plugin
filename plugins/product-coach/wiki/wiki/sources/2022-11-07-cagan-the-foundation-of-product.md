# The Foundation of Product

> **Citation:** [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *The Foundation of Product* · SVPG, Nov 7, 2022 — [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/the-foundation-of-product/). Slim provenance card; raw file stays local.

## Role
**Primary — the SVPG article-form home for the four-criteria definition of an empowered product team.** Cagan's own description of it as "the very nature of product, and the foundation of what it means to be a strong product team." Written as a companion / clarification to his earlier *Two In A Box PM* article which "buried the lede" about the four criteria.

Key material — **the four criteria** for an empowered product team's purpose:
1. Solve **important problems** for our customers or our company
2. In ways that our **users and customers love**
3. With enabling **technology that is just now possible**
4. In ways that **work for our business**

Each criterion has explicit consequences: criterion 1 is why feature roadmaps are broken; criterion 2 is why we do discovery and design; criterion 3 is why engineers must be in discovery (they know what's now possible); criterion 4 is what value-vs-viability trade-offs test. Together they define the empowered team — distinct from delivery teams / feature teams / project teams.
