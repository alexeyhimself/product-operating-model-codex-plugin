# Product Therapy Ep 1 — Coaching Agency: Symptoms and Solutions

> **Citation:** [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] and [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] · *Product Therapy* podcast, Ep 1 · SVPG, March 29, 2024 — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9DAPrL5mdY). This is a **slim provenance card**: the raw transcript stays local (gitignored) and is **never reproduced** here. Follow the link for the real thing.

## Role
**Primary.** The inaugural episode of the *Product Therapy* podcast — SVPG's dedicated forum for the *"behavioral, cultural, and mindset issues holding most teams back"* that the craft-focused canon under-serves. Coins the wiki's [[coaching-agency|agency]] framing: agency = *do people feel they can change their circumstances in meaningful ways, versus feeling the victim of factors beyond their control?* Idiodi's coaching move (the closing "practical tip of the week"): when handed a road-map item, respond *"That's a great idea. I want to make sure we succeed when we do it — what problem are we trying to solve, and how will we know we've succeeded?"* — the disarming turn-a-feature-into-a-problem script.

## Notes for the coach
Cagan opens with a personal disclosure (an eBay assistant's undiagnosed brain tumor) to explain *why* SVPG treats behavioral change as care work, not just technique. Both Cagan and Idiodi are explicit that they are **not therapists**; where the conversation touches depression, anxiety, or grief, they route the listener to professional help. Attribute the agency framing to Idiodi (who chose the topic and drove the coaching arc) and the "wasted potential / Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products" framing to Cagan.
