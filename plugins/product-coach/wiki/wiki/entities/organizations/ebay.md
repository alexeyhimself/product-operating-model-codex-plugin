# eBay

> **Stub.** Part of [[marty-cagan|Cagan]]'s own career history and a recurring reference point in early SVPG writing — not a worked case study like [[spotify]] or [[amazon]].

## Profile
Online marketplace where Cagan served as SVP of Product Management & Design before founding [[svpg|SVPG]]. It appears across the corpus mainly as a source of practices and people rather than as a transformation story.

## Why it's here
- **Origin of the product council.** Cagan credits the concept described in [[2007-02-10-cagan-the-product-council|The Product Council]] (2007) to **Maynard Webb**, eBay's former COO. ⚠️ Note that the article itself is largely **superseded** — it describes a stage-gate governance process the product model rejects; see the contradiction flag on that card before citing it.
- **A recurring comparison point** in early SVPG articles on why different companies legitimately run different product-management models.
