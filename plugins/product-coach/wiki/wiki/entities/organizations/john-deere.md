# John Deere

> American agricultural-equipment manufacturer, founded 1837. In this wiki it is [[chris-jones|Chris Jones]]'s **non-tech exemplar**: the company he reaches for when someone objects that the product model is for software companies.

## Profile
- Industry: agricultural and construction machinery; ~190 years old at the time of the telling.
- Model maturity at the start of Jones's account (c. 2018–19): **legacy throughout** — no true product management discipline, product-owner-as-backlog-administrator, engineers structurally distant from customers.
- Direction of travel: "smart industrial" — automation and efficiency as the unit of customer value, with recurring software revenue alongside hardware.

## Why it's here
An exemplar of the model taking hold in a company that predates the internet, predates computers, and sells a physical asset — and where the resulting innovation (ML-targeted herbicide spraying, invented by an engineer watching a customer through a tractor camera) came from precisely the mechanism the model claims: [[empowered-engineers|engineers with customer context]].

Deere appears in **one** wiki source, a talk, not in the SVPG book canon — see the provenance note on the case study before citing it as heavily as the *TRANSFORMED* companies.
