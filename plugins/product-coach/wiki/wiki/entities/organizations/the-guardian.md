# The Guardian

> British newspaper, founded 1821, trust-funded. In this wiki it is [[marty-cagan|Cagan]]'s favourite **successful-transformation** exemplar — a company the internet nearly destroyed that rebuilt a genuine product capability and then used it to take an opportunity it could not have planned for.

## Profile
- Industry: news media. Revenue base gutted by the internet (classifieds to eBay/Craigslist, jobs and dating verticals gone).
- Governance: owned by a trust; the board's decisive move was appointing **Judy Gibbons** — ex-Apple, ex-Microsoft — as **chair**, not merely as a director. Profiled in [[empowered|*EMPOWERED*]].
- Post-transformation capability: small cross-functional product teams; the flagship iPhone news app was built by five people.

## Why it's here
The worked answer to *"what does transformation buy you?"* — see [[guardian-eyewitness]] for the seven-week iPad story. Cagan's litmus test (*what can you do now that you couldn't before?*) is illustrated with this company more often than any other in the talk corpus.
