# Palace Company (Palace Resorts)

Family-run hospitality group based in Mexico. Brands include **Palace Resorts, Le Blanc Spa Resorts, Moon Palace, Beach Palace**, and others. **~15,000 employees, 6,000+ rooms across 18+ properties in 7 countries** at the time of Product Therapy Ep 28 (Sep 2025).

Owned and run by four brothers (of whom [[anuar-chapur|Anuar Chapur]] is one). Chapur took over technology and drove the transformation to the [[product-operating-model|product operating model]] with [[gabrielle-bufrem|Gabrielle Bufrem]] as embedded coach, starting after Chapur attended [[marty-cagan|Cagan]]'s [[transformed|TRANSFORMED]] workshop in New York.

## Why the wiki cares

Palace is the wiki's first **non-tech, non-US, family-run** transformation exemplar. The story is grounded in [[palace-resorts-transformation]]. In contrast to SaaS transformations like [[datasite-transformation|Datasite]], Palace's context includes:
- **Hospitality operations** — technology has to serve physical experiences (check-in time, room-service delivery, restaurant availability, etc.).
- **Latin America** — coach fit required Spanish-speaking (why Cagan matched Chapur with Bufrem).
- **Family-owned business politics** — Chapur's brothers were the last stakeholders convinced, not the first.
- **Doing-well-but-broken-tech trigger** — record post-COVID years; the transformation trigger was that tech wasn't producing outcomes, not that the business was failing.
