# Palantir

> The company where the **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** practice originated and is most associated — data-analytics software for government (defense, intelligence, law enforcement) and, increasingly, commercial enterprise customers. Named repeatedly, and discussed substantively (not just in passing), in [[2025-09-17-cagan-forward-deployed-engineers|Cagan's Forward Deployed Engineers article]]. Built as an org entity per Batch 10's judgment call: unlike a non-SVPG *book or article* (governed by CLAUDE.md §2's endorsement test), Palantir is a **company discussed at length inside Cagan's own SVPG-authored article** — closer to how [[john-deere]] and [[the-guardian]] earned entity pages from Batch 9's talk corpus than to a cited-but-unendorsed outside work.

## Profile
- **Industry:** data-analytics / AI software, originally defense and intelligence, now also commercial enterprise.
- **Scale:** cited by Cagan (2025) at roughly $400B+ market value, contrasted against Accenture's ~$150B at the time of writing — Cagan's own illustration of how far the FDE-plus-platform-product model has scaled.
- **What makes it relevant here:** Palantir is Cagan's worked example of a company that looks, on the surface, like a custom-solutions/consulting shop (deeply embedded engineers, bespoke-feeling client engagements) but that he explicitly classifies as a genuine **product company** — see "Why it's here" below.

## Why it's here
**Not a case study — no book-length or talk-length narrative exists in the wiki's sources, only one article's worth of material.** Palantir is here as the wiki's clearest illustration of the [[forward-deployed-engineers|Forward Deployed Engineers]] model at scale:

- **Bets on itself, not on a client's spec.** Where a custom-solutions company (Cagan's contrast case: Accenture — cited in passing, not a wiki entity; see the closing note on [[2025-09-17-cagan-forward-deployed-engineers]]'s card) builds exactly what a client specifies and cannot really own the outcome, Palantir's FDEs discover and deliver a solution the company believes will work — for the customer *and* for Palantir's own product line.
- **Scales via platform product, not headcount.** The thing that keeps Palantir's model from collapsing into "thousands of bespoke solutions needing indefinite maintenance" is that its platform product organization continuously generalizes what individual FDEs learn, client by client, into reusable platform capability. Cagan is explicit this is "much easier to say than it is to do."
- **Cagan's own framing, stated directly:** despite the surface resemblance to consulting, he considers Palantir a genuine product company, not a custom-solutions shop — and the article "isn't really about Palantir at all," just illustrated by it.
- **Named as an acknowledgment source, not just a subject:** the article thanks Adam Judelson, an early Palantir FDE, for feedback on the draft — a further-reading lead if the wiki ever goes deeper on Palantir specifically.
