# Hiring Product Managers: Using Product EQ to Go Beyond Culture and Skills

> [[kate-leto|Kate Leto]]'s book on hiring and developing product managers by looking past the standard culture-fit + skills-check to what actually determines their success: **Product EQ** — the emotional-intelligence and human-skills dimensions of PM work.

## What it covers
Practitioner guide for hiring managers, product leaders, and coaches on how to evaluate PM candidates on the human-skills axis that most interviews miss. Argues that the *culture-fit* filter systematically produces monocultures and the *skills-check* filter systematically produces PMs who can execute the mechanics without producing outcomes.

## Key contributions
- **Product EQ** as the frame for what makes a great PM beyond craft skills — the wiki's canonical reframe of PM hiring beyond the standard interview loop.
- **The seven human skills** that Leto teaches (surfaced via [[jon-moore|Moore]] on [[2024-10-24-idiodi-moore-coaching-career-advice|Product Therapy Ep 9]] and reinforced by Leto herself on [[2025-06-12-idiodi-leto-coaching-psychological-safety|Ep 22]]): *emotional intelligence · collaborative problem-solving · critical thinking · trust · agency · time · perseverance*. These are the currency of career development in the model.
- **EQ enables psychological safety** — high-EQ individuals produce the conditions under which teams can develop [[psychological-safety]]; low-EQ individuals systematically block it, however well-intentioned.
