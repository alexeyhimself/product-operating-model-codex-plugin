# Chris Jones

> SVPG partner; co-author with Cagan of *[[empowered|EMPOWERED]]* (product leadership) and *[[transformed|TRANSFORMED]]* (the product operating model). The wiki's canon for the [[product-designer]] competency is his 2016 SVPG article — Cagan repeatedly links to it as the canonical role definition.

## Role here
Primary source for the modern product designer role. Cagan-quoted observation that motivated [[2021-04-27-cagan-product-vs-project-teams|Cagan's "Product vs. Project Teams"]] update: Jones "shared with me recently that he continues to encounter project teams, but I didn't want to believe it was still that prevalent."

**Batch 1:**
- [[2024-07-25-idiodi-jones-coaching-imposter-syndrome]] — Ep 6; the canonical Jones story of learning strategy through the *"definitive point of view in prose"* coaching move; healthy-vs-paralyzing dose framing; the *"sign up for six before you do one"* hack. - [[2024-12-19-idiodi-jones-coaching-time-management]] — Ep 11; the Eisenhower matrix, the pie-chart exercise, the four types of meetings (with the fourth = *build relationship*, added for remote-work reality). Primary source for [[time-management]].
- [[2025-01-23-idiodi-jones-coaching-employee-onboarding]] — Ep 13; Jones interviewing Idiodi on the New-Employee Boot Camp; the public-win discipline learned from Jones's own manager. Primary source for [[hiring-and-onboarding]]'s Idiodi-boot-camp treatment.

**Batch 2:**
- [[2025-04-03-idiodi-jones-coaching-product-myths-part-1]] — Ep 17; the first half of Jones's misconceptions pack. Myths 1–4: *product decides the what / engineering decides the how* (engineers have the best ideas; RACI harms) · *design is downstream* (design is discovery; ~2 dozen prototypes/week) · *hire PMs on domain expertise* (Shreyas Doshi: **domain knowledge − domain dogma**; Jones's security-startup outsider story) · *give customers what they ask for* (Bezos's both-directions rule; the Walkman focus-group say/do gap; **"specials"** with high escalation bar for sales-driven features). Primary root for [[product-myths]].
- [[2025-06-26-idiodi-jones-coaching-product-myths-part-2]] — Ep 23; the second half. Myths 5–9: *discovery gives the answer* (discovery is not a formula; right-size; **grinding metal** as a coaching flag) · *iterate a solution until it succeeds* (running against a clock; *"we have an innovation team"* diagnostic) · *PMs need product owners* (**inside game vs outside game** — must live in a single skull; PO has no career path in a serious product company) · *if you build it they will come* (GTM is part of the product solution; PLG vs sales-led are different products) · *PM is the CEO of their product* (Idiodi's *"opposite of Spider-Man"* — great responsibility, no power). Co-primary root for [[product-myths]].

- [[2024-04-03-jones-moving-to-the-product-operating-model-product-people]] — *Transformed: Moving to a Product Operating Model* (Product People, Apr 2024). The clean ~35-minute model walkthrough. **Root source for [[john-deere-smart-industrial|John Deere]]**, and for his two-part answer to how a transformation is measured.
- [[2024-04-05-jones-product-power-play-pmdojo]] — *Product Power Play* (PMDojo / Bosky Mukherjee, Apr 2024). Career-shaped: luck, imposter syndrome, when to leave a job, go-to-market as part of the product job.

## Biography (from the interviews)

Started as an engineer (CS degree) and, by his own account, was bad at it — *"I was too architectural about it, I couldn't be experimental enough."* Two years making documentary films and corporate training in between, which is where he says he first developed a sense of story, design and the experience of the person consuming the thing. Ran a small product-design agency for roughly eight years around the dawn of the web — where he first met [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]], then VP of product management at Netscape and a client. Moved into product management proper at **Vontu**, the company that defined the data-loss-prevention category; led product there through the Symantec acquisition, then across Symantec's enterprise portfolio, then at **Lookout**. Joined SVPG in **November 2015** at Cagan's invitation.

**Vontu is the company he says formed him.** A co-founder once stopped him mid-complaint: *"you're going to spend the rest of your career trying to recreate what you have at this place."* Jones says it turned out to be right — the company understood empowerment and active management, and the alumni network persists.

**On what he is proudest of:** not the products. *"Where the people who were on my team are now."* And: *"once you become a real leader of product, your product is the organization you're building."*
