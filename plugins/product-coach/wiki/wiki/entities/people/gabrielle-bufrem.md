# Gabrielle Bufrem

Product and leadership coach who partners with executives and teams worldwide. Formerly ran product design at a startup; her transition to coaching came after meeting [[marty-cagan|Marty Cagan]] and being told by him — after she'd said she wasn't in love with any other products enough to take another operating role — *"you told me you want to do this thing, so I don't think you're going to fall in love again. You're going to have to do what you told me you wanted to do."*

Bufrem is a **wiki-primary voice on the coach-craft and on the pilot-team pattern**. Her background as an ex-designer + product leader shows up in the way she coaches: heavy on trust-building, live-role-play feedback, and *"leaving space for magic."* She speaks Spanish and Portuguese, which is how Cagan matched her with the [[palace-resorts-transformation|Palace Resorts transformation]] as its embedded coach.

Not an SVPG partner — external product coach, similar wiki-primary-adjacent status to [[teresa-torres|Torres]], [[kate-leto|Leto]], [[elias-lieberich|Lieberich]], and [[marcus-castenfors|Castenfors]].

## Core teachings surfaced in the wiki

- **Coach / advisor / mentor / manager — the crisp distinctions** ([[product-coaching]] via Ep 31).
- **"Everyone deserves a coach"** (not "needs" — deserves). The best players in the world have multiple coaches.
- **Trust as the currency.** Meet in person for anything big. Remember the small things that matter to them.
- **"Leave some space for magic."** Clients often want something different by the end than what they set out to want.
- **Coaching feedback as questions, not judgment.** Reserve judgment → acknowledge the emotion → ask questions that guide to their own clarity → never write it for them. *"My job is not to fish for you. My job is to teach you how to fish."*
- **Tennis vs fresco ball metaphor** for workplace communication. Tennis = play to beat the opponent; fresco ball = rally with the partner — hit the ball so they can respond.
- **Pilot team as poster child, not prototype** ([[pilot-teams]] via Ep 34) — the wiki's canonical framing.
- **Trust debt at start** — you're not starting from zero trust, you're starting from debt because they've tried other things.
- **Swim team (project model) vs soccer team (empowered model)** — "you can't play soccer without the goalie; there is no playing alone."
- **The pilot lives on an island — no bridge, just a boat.** Protect it from the mainland.
- **The two muscles a transformation must build simultaneously:** teams learn to solve problems (they're good at delivering); leaders learn to decide which problems to solve with context (they're good at control).
- **"Exec tech love language is Excel."** Well-populated dashboards are how execs receive progress.
- **Dream Team framing.** Pilot-team members carry a responsibility: what you learn, you teach.
- **Anti-patterns** she names: pilot theater, pilot-team factory, sacred golden child.
- **Three areas Bufrem assesses** when coaching a transformation: how you *build*, how you *solve problems*, how you *decide which problems to solve* — matches the SVPG *changing how you build / changing how you solve problems / changing which problems you solve* triad.
