# Anuar Chapur

Chief Product and Technology Officer at **The Palace Company** (Palace Resorts / Le Blanc / Moon Palace / Beach Palace and other properties) — a Mexico-based family-run hospitality group with ~15,000 employees and 6,000+ rooms across seven countries. Member of the founding family.

Chapur is the wiki's exemplar of a **non-tech-background, family-business transformation sponsor**. Not a career technology leader — took over the ~150-person product/tech organization not from expertise but from conviction, after realizing that Palace's software was not driving results despite the business doing well. Attended [[marty-cagan|Cagan]]'s [[transformed|TRANSFORMED]] workshop in New York; Cagan introduced him to [[gabrielle-bufrem]] as a Spanish-speaking coach who could work with his team. Two years in, the transformation has produced measurable business outcomes (booking-engine conversion 1% → 5%+; $600K in new bookings in 2 months from one feature — see [[palace-resorts-transformation]]).

## What he brings to the wiki

- The **"no plan B"** commitment answer — Bufrem's first-question test for a transformation sponsor.
- The **"if this is going to work, you have to come here — you are not a screen on Zoom"** framing for how a coach earns local trust in a non-US, non-tech context.
- The **family-company transformation politics** frame — his brothers were the last people he convinced. His principle: *"protect the company from ourselves if we're ever in disagreement."*
- The **"stop having thoughts of your own and start trusting Cagan's thoughts on everything that has to be done — then get a coach who has been there, done that"** advice to other leaders starting the journey.
- **"I hired a team that allows me to be bold"** — his own summary of the transformation's leadership shift.
