# Marcus Castenfors

Product coach and author of **10 Anti-Patterns When Moving to the Product Model** — a field guide on why product transformations stall, backslide, or collapse under pressure. External to SVPG. Has spent years helping organizations make the shift to the product model, primarily in European enterprises.

Castenfors is the **wiki-primary voice on transformation anti-patterns as a named catalog**. Where [[lea-hickman|Hickman]]'s treatment is the messy-middle field-battle-stories view of transformation, Castenfors provides the *named* vocabulary — the loop of failure, the air sandwich, the three value buckets, the sandwich-in-a-bubble trap.

## Core teachings surfaced in the wiki

- **The loop of transformation failure** — six-step failure loop from *"let's give teams the opportunity to innovate"* through *"patience is a virtue"* to *"back to micromanagement, oh crap that stifles innovation."* Root cause: coaches failed to teach the new capabilities, leaders stepped too far away, teams failed to be transparent.
- **Losing momentum** — the anti-pattern that stalks every transformation; doubters need to see results.
- **Define the big *why*** — the purpose, objectives, vision of what's better than the status quo. *"You can sense in some transformations there is lack of energy."*
- **The air sandwich** — clear vision at the top, clear backlog at the bottom, a big question mark in the middle. The product model connects the dots.
- **The 17 North Stars problem** — *"how can you make a decision on what's most important when you have 17 North Stars? It is not a North Star at that point."*
- **Don't transform product-and-tech in a bubble** — sales/marketing/CS/HR/legal/finance all need to be brought along.
- **Three buckets of product work:** new value / existing value / protect value. All must be visible. Pilot teams need to work on new-or-existing-value; if swamped with protect-value work, expectation mismatch is guaranteed.
- **Dependencies are the root of all evil.** Slowness like tar.
- **Do you have the vehicle for your vision?** Team topology, capability, delivery infrastructure = the car.
- **Transformation valley of despair.** Counters: discovery demos to build momentum; leaders moving out of their comfort zone.
- **The golden rule of transformations:** meet-them-where-they-are + show results + name the anti-patterns as they appear so people don't slip into them unconsciously.
