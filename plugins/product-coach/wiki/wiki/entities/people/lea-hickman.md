# Lea Hickman

> SVPG partner and *[[transformed|TRANSFORMED]]* co-author, best known for leading **Adobe's transformation** from boxed Creative Suite to the Creative Cloud subscription — one of the canonical product-model transformations. Co-author with Cagan of the *Keys to Successful Transformation* article the wiki leans on for [[transformation-readiness]] and [[model-maturity]]. By Batch 3, the wiki's most-cited *Product Therapy* co-host after Idiodi himself — six episodes spanning coaching craft (coaching-up), execution tools (roadmaps, strategy jumpstarts), and transformation practice.

## Role here
Root author (with Cagan) of the **ten keys to successful transformation**. In [[2024-02-14-cagan-getting-leadership-up-to-speed-melissa-perri|Getting Leadership Up to Speed]], Cagan says: "Lea and I wrote an article together called *Keys to Successful Transformation* — we went through all the companies that tried to transform, [found] the small subset that succeeded, and came up with ten things that made the difference," the first being **CEO responsibility**. Her Adobe experience is one of the lived transformation case studies behind [[transformed|TRANSFORMED]].

**The transformation she led (now confirmed and detailed):** *TRANSFORMED* Part IX is Hickman's first-person account of the **[[adobe-creative-cloud-transformation|Creative Suite → Creative Cloud]] transformation** at Adobe (2007–2014). Appointed head of product management for Creative Cloud after a senior-leadership offsite revealed misaligned visions across product categories. Worked with CEO Shantanu Narayen, CFO Mark Garrett, CTO Kevin Lynch, SVPs David Wadhwani and Johnny Loiacono, and head of engineering Kevin Stewart. Authored the **Marissa visiontype** — the aspirational day-in-the-life narrative that aligned executives, board, employees, and customers. Result: 6M → 26M customers, $2B → $11.5B revenue, market cap $13B → $269B. Cagan calls it *"one of the most financially successful transformations in industry history."*

## *Product Therapy* podcast — co-host with [[christian-idiodi|Idiodi]] (2024–)
Hickman appears on 6 episodes of the *Product Therapy* podcast (Batch 1 · Eps 4, 8; Batch 2 · Eps 15, 18; Batch 3 · Eps 27, 32, 37, 42 — four episode cards, two of which cross-reference each other):

**Batch 1:**
- [[2024-05-31-idiodi-hickman-coaching-trust]] — Ep 4; the four dimensions of trust; the new-hire trust asymmetry; the *"you can't build trust over a document"* line; the mala-Sharma-at-Adobe *"paint a picture bigger than any individual team"* frame; the public attribution of the boot-camp origin to Idiodi ~8+ years before the recording. Primary source for [[trust-over-control]]'s four-dimensions section.
- [[2024-09-26-idiodi-hickman-coaching-stakeholder-collaboration]] — Ep 8; the voice-vs-vote definition; the Merrill acquisition stop-sign-vs-yield-sign investigation; the Rule of Improv as the stakeholder-idea-handling default. Primary source for [[stakeholder-relationship-health]]'s voice-vs-vote section.

**Batch 2:**
- [[2025-02-20-idiodi-hickman-coaching-remote-collaboration]] — Ep 15; the **rules of proximity** (optimize for the biggest gap; Bangalore/SF worked example); the *"documentation is about capturing a conversation, not the vehicle for alignment"* rule; the false-trust trap in remote settings; disagree-and-commit as culture; the HR-driven fix of tying every VP's objectives to shared transformation outcomes. Primary root for [[remote-collaboration]].
- [[2025-04-17-idiodi-hickman-coaching-change-management]] — Ep 18; the *"never coach a change-management department — coach the leaders"* rule; delegation as the #1 change-management anti-pattern; big-bang vs incremental (the acquisition-is-different rule); the middle-management resistance layer; training → coaching shift; measure results not activity. Enriches [[transformation]], [[transformation-readiness]], [[keys-to-successful-transformation]].

**Batch 3:**
- [[2025-09-04-idiodi-hickman-coaching-up]] — Ep 27; **primary root for the new [[coaching-up]] concept page** — distinct from a manager coaching an IC. *Coaching up* is exposing a leader to information they might not see, with no career-progression motive: *"It has nothing to do with career progression... it's about helping someone get exposed to information they might not be open to."* Gives the if-your-manager-doesn't-coach-you sequence (ask directly · model the framework you want · celebrate when they try), the jiu-jitsu move for the always-right manager (*"help me break this down"*), and the name-the-pattern-with-positive-intent technique for the disengaged/checked-out manager — including Hickman's own anecdote of Cagan telling her *"I'm surprised you have waited so long"* about escalating one.
- [[2025-11-13-idiodi-hickman-coaching-roadmaps]] — Ep 32; Hickman's canonical shape for the **outcome-based roadmap** (outcome → key results → feature *candidates*, not features) and the operational rule *"I would never commit to a date. I'd commit to a timeframe."* Names the four roadmap anti-patterns (stakeholder-driven, executive/competitor-driven, capacity-fill, feature-factory) and the executive-pushback coaching move *"change what you control... control the strategic context."* Introduces the **`HIC:`** (high-integrity commitment) notation for flagging the rare firm-date row on an otherwise outcome-based roadmap, and the transitional-roadmap technique for migrating a feature roadmap without tearing it up. Major deepening of [[outcome-based-roadmap]] and [[high-integrity-commitments]].
- [[2026-02-19-idiodi-hickman-coaching-transformations]] — Ep 37; field-battle-stories treatment of the *messy middle* of transformation. The mostly-fine-but-blind-spots trigger (revenue's fine, but *"we weren't driving the level of innovation we could in the past."*); the role-play opener for convincing a doing-well business leader to change; the three dimensions of the product operating model restated for a general audience (how you build / how you discover / how you decide); and Hickman's *"installation, not adoption"* diagnosis for transformations that just rename Product Owners to Product Managers and call it done. **First names the Strategy Jumpstart** as her primary strategy-creation tool, pointing forward to Ep 42.
- [[2026-07-16-idiodi-hickman-strategy-jumpstarts]] — Ep 42; **primary root for the new [[strategy-jumpstart]] framework page** — the SVPG method for creating or refining a company's product strategy in the room. Room composition (8–10 decision-makers with real agency, no proxies); the homework/insight-gathering that precedes it; the **false-constraints** move (*"if you only had 10 engineers, where would you focus?"*); **questioning the sacred** teams nobody's allowed to touch; **tumbling the rocks together** as the necessary in-room friction; outputs = a prioritized problem list with **quantified** (not just directional) measures of success; **one priority list across the whole org**; and the discipline that only new data — not politics — warrants revisiting the strategy.

Together, the Batch 3 episodes make Hickman the wiki's primary voice on two adjacent but distinct crafts: **coaching upward inside a reporting relationship** (Ep 27) and **coaching a leadership team through the hardest strategic trade-offs** (Ep 42) — with the roadmap (Ep 32) and transformation (Ep 37) episodes as the connective tissue between vision-level strategy and team-level execution.
