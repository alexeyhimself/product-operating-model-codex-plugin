# Mike Fisher

CEO of **MyFitnessPal**; former CTO of **Etsy**. Long-time engineering-leadership author and coach. Writes a newsletter on engineering leadership, technology strategy, and organizational scaling.

Fisher is treated in this wiki as a **wiki-primary voice on AI's impact on engineering work and on cultural debt** — two topics where his combined operator seat (CEO of an AI-forward consumer app) + engineering-leadership background make him uniquely positioned. His frame consistently pushes back on the *AI-means-fewer-people* narrative (Jevons paradox for product) and on the *culture-is-HR's-job* framing (culture is a C-suite responsibility).

Not an SVPG partner — external guest.

## Core teachings surfaced in the wiki

**On AI and engineering work** ([[ai-and-product-teams]], [[engineers]] via Ep 40):
- **Jevons paradox for product** — more efficiency creates more demand, not less.
- **Radiologists grew 17%** after AI was meant to replace them.
- **Programming vs coding** — programming (thinking) survives, coding (typing) doesn't. Architecture, systems-level thinking, asking the right questions become the spike for engineers.
- **The one-pizza rule** — updating Bezos's two-pizza rule for the agentic-programming era.
- **FinOps for AI — the token bill that eats your savings.** New role: someone specialized in optimization of prompts and agentic agents.
- **Hyper-personalization** as the new expectation baseline.
- **Product judgment matters MORE, not less** as throughput explodes.
- **"Customers, trust, judgment stay stubbornly human."**
- **"Learn the fundamentals, then embrace the tools."**

**On cultural debt** ([[cultural-debt]], [[product-culture]] via Ep 43):
- **Culture = the system that governs how people work together.** Cultural debt = the hidden cost of neglecting that system. Like tech debt, some is inevitable; the problem is when you ignore the interest.
- **What you reward always beats what you say.** Wells Fargo as the case study.
- **Signals cultural debt is unhealthy:** silence in rooms where there used to be debate; increased *"what he really meant"* translation work; losing great people; customer impact.
- **The Boeing case** — decades of cultural debt → 737 Max crashes → Alaska Airlines door-bolts years later — *"shows how long cultural debt can last."*
- **Blameless postmortem for cultural failures.** *"99.99% of people come to work to do good work."*
- **Culture is a C-suite responsibility, not an HR problem.**
- **"Avoidance disguised as kindness"** — the No More Mr. Nice Guy frame.
- **The MyFitnessPal "School of Fish"** — the 10-minute leader-speaks-at-every-all-hands practice Fisher installed as a way to pay down cultural debt after the company skipped a fall gathering.
