# Jon Moore

> SVPG partner and co-author of *[[transformed|TRANSFORMED]]* (with Cagan, Hickman, Idiodi, and Chris Jones). The wiki's canon for the three transformation dimensions — **changing how you build / solve problems / decide which problems to solve** — comes from his 2022 SVPG transformation series, **rooted in his earlier May 2022 *Transformation Defined* article** that introduced the framing.

## Role here
**Root author** of the [[2022-05-31-moore-transformation-defined|three-dimensions framing]] of transformation. The Sep 2022 deep-dives are the per-dimension expansions. Per the [[2023-02-20-cagan-transformed-announcement|TRANSFORMED announcement]], Moore had been the original second author of *TRANSFORMED* before passing the project to Cagan; his transformation series previewed the book's framing.

**The transformation he led before joining SVPG (now confirmed):** *TRANSFORMED* Part V is Moore's first-person account of the **[[trainline-transformation|Trainline transformation]]** — KKR bought Trainline in January 2015 believing it was undervalued; Moore joined as CPO alongside CEO Clare Gilmartin (from eBay) and CTO Mark Holt. Four years later Trainline IPO'd at ~£2B on the London Stock Exchange (from KKR's <£500M purchase). Ch 49 continues the arc with an innovation story (price prediction). The Trainline account is the wiki's clearest end-to-end transformation walk-through of Moore's own three-dimensions framework.

## *Product Therapy* podcast — co-host with [[christian-idiodi|Idiodi]] (2024–)
Moore appears on 3 episodes of the *Product Therapy* podcast (Batch 1 · Eps 5, 9; Batch 2 · Ep 21):

**Batch 1:**
- [[2024-06-27-idiodi-moore-coaching-ethics]] — Ep 5; the ethics-as-fifth-risk framing; the China Communications-launch story on psychological safety; *"good ethics is good business"*; diversity as upstream of ethics. Primary source for [[product-ethics]].
- [[2024-10-24-idiodi-moore-coaching-career-advice]] — Ep 9; the *"master a discipline before moving into product"* claim; the Cagan-as-mentor arc (unsent email → CPO tough-love call); *"don't rush team-level jobs"*; the *"create value"* answer to *"how do I stay relevant?"*. Primary source for [[product-career-development]].

**Batch 2:**
- [[2025-05-29-idiodi-moore-coaching-vision]] — Ep 21; the wiki's most complete podcast-form teaching on product vision. **Mission = why · vision = what over 3–5 years · strategy = how · roadmap = when/what.** The three core ingredients (validated problems + reflects corporate strategy + glimpse of money). **The CFO test** as the true test of a strong vision. Moore's Trainline **deep-then-broad validation method** (deep with small set of customers per geography, then broad validation). Time-horizon compression under GenAI (12–18 month visions now common). Enrichment source for [[product-vision]], [[vision-quality]], [[trainline-transformation]], [[kaiser-permanente-get-care-now]] (Get Care Now case).
