# Kate Leto

> Executive coach and author who has shaped the wiki's treatment of the *human skills* half of product work — the emotional intelligence, career development, and psychological safety without which the model's mechanics do not compound.

## Contributions & positions
- **Product EQ** as the frame for what makes a great product manager beyond craft skills. Leto's book *[[hiring-product-managers|Hiring Product Managers: Using Product EQ to Go Beyond Culture and Skills]]* argues that hiring and developing PMs on their *culture-fit + skills-check* alone systematically misses what actually determines their success — the emotional-intelligence dimension.
- **The seven human skills** Leto teaches (via [[jon-moore|Jon Moore]] on [[2024-10-24-idiodi-moore-coaching-career-advice|Product Therapy Ep 9]]): *emotional intelligence · collaborative problem-solving · critical thinking · trust · agency · time · perseverance*. This has become the wiki's canonical reframe of career development from *"become a better product manager"* to *"grow the seven skills that make PM work possible."* See [[product-career-development]].
- **Trust ≠ psychological safety** — Leto's crispest teaching (from [[2025-06-12-idiodi-leto-coaching-psychological-safety|Product Therapy Ep 22]]): *"trust is dyadic — between two people. Psychological safety is amongst a team."* You can trust every individual on your team and still not feel safe speaking up in a group meeting. See [[psychological-safety]].
- **The hero complex** as the primary blocker to psychological safety — leaders trained by an unwritten rule that they must always know, always save the day. Leto's coaching move: shift focus from *"who is right"* to *"what can we learn / what does the team think."*
- **EQ enables psychological safety; PS is the outcome.** EQ is the individual capability; PS is the team-level phenomenon.
