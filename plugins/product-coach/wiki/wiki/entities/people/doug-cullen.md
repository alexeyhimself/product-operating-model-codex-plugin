# Doug Cullen

> Sales leader who partnered with [[thomas-fredell|Thomas Fredell]] (CTPO) to drive the 50-year-old Merrill Corporation's transformation into Datasite — the practitioner voice on how sales and product build trust during a multi-year platform build. Long-time colleague of [[christian-idiodi|Christian Idiodi]] from Interlinks days.

## Contributions & positions
- **The culture-bank metaphor** — Cullen's crispest single-line contribution to the wiki's transformation canon: *"you have to have something in the culture bank if you're going to make a withdrawal from it."* The Datasite One launch failure (see [[datasite-transformation]]) was the moment the company had to draw down what it had accumulated in trust — and the fact that it worked was proof the deposits had been made. See [[transformation]].
- **The extended trio** — jointly with [[christian-idiodi|Idiodi]], extended the standard product trio (PM + designer + engineer) to pull in sales and service management when needed, keeping the sales team engaged as supporters rather than skeptics during a long platform build with no product to show for months. See [[cross-functional-partnering]] and [[datasite-transformation]].
- **Right people on the bus** (Jim Collins) as the transformation-leadership discipline — Cullen inherited *"excellent talent that had not been empowered,"* rather than needing the wholesale management-wipe Fredell needed on the product/engineering side.
- **Force collaboration outside the business area** — Cullen's transformation-leader habit of hosting sales leaders at his home; his predecessor Todd hosted every sales leader at his childhood Cleveland home 3 weeks after Cullen joined. Human-connection ritual as trust-building.
