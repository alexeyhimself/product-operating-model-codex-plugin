# Josh Kerievsky

> Founder and CEO of **Industrial Logic**, and co-author with [[marty-cagan|Cagan]] of [[2024-08-13-cagan-kerievsky-the-product-model-in-outsourcing|"The Product Model in Outsourcing"]] (Aug 2024) — the third and final article in SVPG's "probing the edges" series. Author of *[[the-joy-of-agility|The Joy of Agility]]* (2023), on the SVPG-endorsed reading list.

## Why this page exists — a judgment call, made explicitly
Kerievsky is an unusual case for this wiki, and Batch 10 (2026-09-06) resolved it deliberately rather than by silent default. He clears **two independent grounds** for a person entity page — either alone would justify a stub:

1. **SVPG co-authorship.** He co-wrote an article published on svpg.com with Cagan, the same way [[stacey-langer]] and [[thomas-fredell]] earned stub pages purely for co-authoring SVPG articles ([[2024-08-07-cagan-langer-the-product-model-in-government|Langer]], [[2025-01-21-cagan-fredell-ma-and-the-product-model|Fredell]]). CLAUDE.md §2's non-SVPG-boundary test — "Cagan must have explicitly endorsed the specific work" — was written for outside sources being *cited*; it does not straightforwardly cover someone credited as a **co-author of SVPG's own content**. Precedent treats an SVPG co-author as a contributor to the canon itself, not an outside source subject to the endorsement test — this page follows that precedent rather than stretching §2 in either direction. (See the clarifying sentence added to CLAUDE.md §2 the same day, prompted by this exact case.)
2. **His own book independently clears the §2 endorsement bar anyway.** *[[the-joy-of-agility|The Joy of Agility]]* (2023) is on SVPG's official *[[svpg-product-operating-model-introduction|Product Operating Model: An Introduction]]* reading list — the same list, and the same kind of endorsement, that gives [[continuous-discovery-habits|Teresa Torres's book]] its `primary-adjacent` / `cagan-endorsed` status. This was true before Batch 10 (the stub already carried `recommended_by: [[svpg]]` and `recommended_in: [[svpg-product-operating-model-introduction]]`), but its frontmatter hadn't been brought in line with the Torres template until this batch — see that page's own note.

Ground 1 alone would be enough (co-authorship, independent of the book). Ground 2 alone would also be enough (the book, independent of co-authorship). Both apply here simultaneously, which is what makes this case worth documenting rather than deciding by default.

## Contributions & positions
- **Co-author, [[2024-08-13-cagan-kerievsky-the-product-model-in-outsourcing|The Product Model in Outsourcing]]** (Aug 2024) — the article reframes the outsourcing-agency client relationship from "here's a spec, build it for a fixed price" to "discover and deliver an effective solution, time-and-materials, renewed period over period," with Kerievsky's Industrial Logic perspective on agencies serving product-model-minded clients.
- **Real agility over ritual** — *The Joy of Agility*'s thesis, aligned with the model's [[principles-over-process|principles-over-process]] stance: being quick, adaptable, and resourceful, not compliant with a fixed set of Agile ceremonies.
